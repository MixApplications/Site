5. Data Retention and Deletion Policy

We operate a serverless architecture for user profiles. We do not require user account creation, nor do we store any identifiable user databases on our own servers. However, we ensure compliance with data deletion requirements regarding third-party analytics:

  • Right to Request Deletion: You have the right to request to delete personal data that we collect. Because user data is automatically deleted within 90 days by our analytics providers (like Firebase), no manual deletion is typically required.
  • Local Data & Ad IDs: You can completely delete all local data by uninstalling the application. You can clear associated tracking data immediately by resetting your device's Advertising ID from your Android system settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID).

6. European Privacy Rights (GDPR & UK GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, or Switzerland, you possess the following rights:

  • Right to Access and Correct: You have the right to request access to and correct the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Delete: You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority if you believe our processing of your personal data violates applicable data protection laws.
  • Consent Management: You can withdraw your consent for advertising tracking at any time by accessing the "Privacy Settings" option within the application's Help or Settings menu.

7. United States Privacy Rights (CCPA, CPRA, VCDPA, CPA, CTDPA, UCPA)

Residents of California, Virginia (VCDPA), Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, and other applicable US states possess specific privacy rights. We operate under an opt-out model for these jurisdictions.

  • Right to Know About Sales: You have the right to know whether your personal data is sold. (We do not sell personal data for monetary value).
  • Right to Know About Sharing: You have the right to know whether your personal data is shared. (We share Identifiers, Location, and App Interactions with ad networks for targeted advertising).
  • Right to Opt-Out: You have the right to opt-out of allowing the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal data for targeted advertising.
  • How to Opt-Out: You can exercise your right to opt-out by clicking the "Privacy Settings" button located within the application's Help menu and adjusting your preferences.
  • Right to Delete: You have the right to request to delete personal data that we collect.
