1. General Overview & Zero-Upload Policy

We believe that your private files and data should remain completely yours. This Privacy Policy is built on the principle of transparency. Our applications are designed to perform heavy operational utility tasks on your mobile device without relying on remote network hosting or databases for your processed content.

2. App Permissions & Strictly Local Processing

Depending on the specific application you use, we may request certain device permissions (such as access to Storage, Media, or connected USB/OTG devices) strictly to provide the core utility features of the application.

  • Zero Upload Policy: We strictly declare that any files, media, or data processed through our utility features are handled 100% locally on your device. We do not upload, collect, or transmit your personal files, documents, or disk contents to our servers or any third-party servers.
  • Background Operations: To ensure data safety and device stability, our applications do not run processing tasks in the background; all operations pause when the app is minimized to protect your device resources.

3. In-App Purchases and Virtual Rewards

Our applications offer a virtual reward system ("Coins") and a premium "Pro Version". Coins can be earned for free by watching rewarded advertisements or purchased using real money. Please note the following regarding our in-app purchases:

  • Payment Processing: All financial transactions and in-app purchases are securely processed by the Google Play Billing system. We do not directly collect, process, or store your credit card, bank details, or financial information on our servers.
  • Data Storage & Purchases: As stated within the application, coin balances are stored strictly locally on your device and are not shareable between devices. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data will permanently delete your coin balance. However, the "Pro Version" purchase is securely linked to your Google account and remains unaffected by local data clearance.

4. Data Retention and Deletion Policy (No Server Storage)

We operate a serverless architecture for user profiles. We do not require user account creation, nor do we store or maintain any personal data, files, or identifiable user profiles on our own servers. Therefore, we cannot manually delete specific user records upon request, as they simply do not exist in our systems.

  • Automatic Deletion (Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics): Any anonymous usage, diagnostic, or crash data collected via third-party services like Firebase is aggregated and subject to automatic deletion after a strict, predefined retention period set by Google. Specifically, Crashlytics data is retained for up to 90 days, and Firebase Analytics data is retained for 2 months before being automatically and permanently deleted.
  • Local App Data: You can completely delete all local data, settings, and permissions associated with our Services at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the application from your device settings.
  • Advertising Data: You can reset or delete your advertising profile and clear associated tracking data at any time by resetting your device's Advertising ID from your Android system settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID).
