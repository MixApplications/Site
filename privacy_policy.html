This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications and services developed and published by MixApplications ("we," "us," or "our"). By downloading or using any of our applications, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. General Overview & Local-First Processing

Our applications are designed to perform utility tasks locally on your device. The content of the files, disk images, and media you process through our utility features (formatting, burning, conversion, and similar operations) is handled 100% locally — we do not upload, collect, or transmit the contents of your personal files or disk images to our servers.

Two limited exceptions apply, described in full in the sections referenced below: (1) if a file-access error or app crash occurs, our crash-reporting tool may record diagnostic information that includes the file path or file name involved, as part of standard crash/error logging (see Section 10); and (2) when you complete an in-app purchase, a purchase identifier and a Google-issued verification token — not file content — are sent to our own server-side function to confirm the purchase with Google Play (see Section 2).

2. In-App Purchases and Virtual Rewards

All financial transactions and in-app purchases are securely processed by the Google Play Billing system. We do not directly collect or store your credit card or financial information. Virtual coin balances are stored strictly locally on your device.

To confirm a purchase's validity — for example, to restore your Pro entitlement after reinstalling the app — the product identifier and a Google-issued purchase token are sent to our own server-side verification function, which checks them against Google Play's records. This function does not persistently store the data it receives (see Section 3).

3. Data Retention and Deletion Policy

We do not maintain a persistent database of user profiles. When you make a purchase, the purchase identifier and verification token are processed transiently by our server-side verification function solely to confirm the purchase with Google Play; they are not written to a database, though — like any server request — they may briefly appear in our cloud infrastructure provider's operational logs. Anonymous usage data collected via third-party SDKs is retained for a limited period and then automatically deleted: Crashlytics data (crash stack traces and associated identifiers) is retained for 90 days, and Firebase Analytics data (both event data and user data) is retained for 2 months.

Right to Request Deletion: You have the right to request deletion of personal data we process. Uninstalling the app permanently removes all data stored locally on your device, including your coin balance. To request deletion of data tied to SDK-level identifiers (such as your Advertising ID), you may reset that identifier in your device's Android settings, or contact us using the details in Section 11.

4. European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

This section outlines the rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for users in the EU and the European Economic Area (EEA). If you are located in the EU/EEA, your personal data is processed in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

We process information based on the following legal bases: Consent (for advertising/analytics), Legitimate Interests (for fixing bugs/fraud prevention), and Contractual Necessity. You possess the following rights:

  • The right to request access to the personal data that we collect.
  • The right to correct inaccurate personal data.
  • The right to request to delete personal data that we collect.
  • The right to withdraw your consent at any time.
  • The right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority regarding the processing of your data.

5. United States Privacy Rights (CCPA)

This section outlines the rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) for users in California, as well as residents of Virginia (VCDPA), Colorado (CPA), Connecticut (CTDPA), and Utah (UCPA).

  • Right to Know About Data Sales: You have the right to know whether your personal data is sold or shared.
  • Right to Know: You have the right to request disclosure of what personal data we collect and share.
  • Right to Delete: You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Data Sale: You have the right to opt-out of the sale of your personal data.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Data Sharing: You have the right to opt-out of the sharing of your personal data for targeted advertising.

How to Opt-Out of the Sale or Sharing of Your Data

We provide methods for users to opt-out. To exercise your right to opt-out of the sale or sharing of your personal data, you can use any of the following methods:

  • In-App Opt-Out (when shown): Some users will see a "Privacy Settings" button in the app's Help (?) menu. This button is shown automatically when our consent management platform determines a privacy choice is required for your account, and lets you withdraw consent for ad personalization directly. If you do not see this button, our systems have determined a regional consent prompt is not currently required for you — you can still opt out using either of the methods below.
  • Email Request: Submit an opt-out request by sending an email to our Data Protection Officer at islam.saad2005@gmail.com.
  • Device Settings: Reset or delete your device's Advertising ID from your Android system settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

6. Brazil Privacy Rights (LGPD)

Under the Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD), users in Brazil have the right to request access, correction, anonymization, blocking, or deletion of personal data. You can exercise these rights using the opt-out methods described above.

7. Other Global Privacy Rights

We aim to honor data subject rights for users in other jurisdictions with comprehensive privacy laws, including Canada (PIPEDA), South Korea (PIPA), Japan (APPI), China (PIPL), South Africa (POPIA), India (DPDPA), and Australia (Privacy Act 1988), to the extent required by applicable law. Where those laws apply to you, you may request access, correction, or deletion of your data using the contact details in Section 11. This section describes rights we extend on request and is not a representation of independently certified or audited compliance with each named framework.

8. Age-Appropriate Experience & Children's Privacy

This application's declared target audience on Google Play is users aged 18 and older, and our Services are not designed or marketed to attract children. Because app stores cannot fully prevent younger users from installing an app, we have built in additional safeguards for the possibility that a user is a minor: where available, we use signals from Google Play's Age Signals API (see Section 10) to provide an age-appropriate advertising experience — showing non-personalized, contextual ads rather than personalized ads to users who are identified as being under 18 — and we tag ad requests accordingly using Google's industry-standard "under age of consent" mechanism. In-app purchases attempted by a user identified this way require an additional parental-gate verification step before they can complete.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is necessary to provide these age-appropriate safeguards, and the underlying age signal is not stored long-term by us (see Section 10).
