Privacy Policy

Last Updated and Effective Date: June 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications and services developed and published by MixApplications ("we," "us," or "our"). We are deeply committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This document outlines how we collect, use, and protect your data across our Services.

By downloading, accessing, or using any of our applications, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue the use of our Services.

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

A. App Permissions & Strictly Local Processing

Depending on the application, we may request certain device permissions (such as access to Storage or connected USB/OTG devices) strictly to provide core utility features.

  • Zero Upload Policy: We strictly declare that any files, media, or data processed through our utility features are handled 100% locally on your device. We do not upload, collect, or transmit your personal files or disk contents to our servers or any third-party servers.
  • Background Operations: To ensure data safety and device stability, our applications do not run processing tasks in the background; all operations pause when the app is minimized.

B. Information Collected Automatically

While we do not collect personal identification details (like your name or email), we automatically collect specific technical data to operate, monetize, and improve our Services. This includes Device/App IDs, IP addresses, App activity (Product Interaction), and Diagnostic logs.

C. Data We Explicitly DO NOT Collect

We do not collect, process, or store sensitive personal information, including but not limited to: hardware Android IDs (SSAID), biometric data, precise GPS geolocation, or personal communications.

D. In-App Purchases and Virtual Rewards

Our applications offer a virtual reward system ("Coins") and a premium "Pro Version". Coins can be earned for free by watching rewarded advertisements or purchased using real money. Please note the following regarding our in-app purchases:

  • Payment Processing: All financial transactions and in-app purchases are securely processed by the Google Play Billing system. We do not directly collect, process, or store your credit card, bank details, or financial information on our servers.
  • Data Storage & Purchases: As stated within the application, coin balances are stored strictly locally on your device and are not shareable between devices. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data will permanently delete your coin balance. However, the "Pro Version" purchase is securely linked to your Google account and remains unaffected by local data clearance.

3. Data Retention and Deletion Policy (No Server Storage)

We operate a serverless architecture for user profiles. We do not require user account creation, nor do we store or maintain any personal data, files, or identifiable user profiles on our own servers. Therefore, we cannot manually delete specific user records upon request, as they simply do not exist in our systems.

  • Automatic Deletion (Firebase): As outlined in the SDK table above, diagnostic and crash data collected via Firebase are subject to strict, predefined retention periods set by Google before being automatically and permanently deleted.
  • Local App Data: You can completely delete all local data, settings, and permissions associated with our Services at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the application from your device settings.
  • Advertising Data: You can reset your advertising profile and clear associated tracking data at any time by resetting your device's Advertising ID from your Android system settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID).

4. European Privacy Rights (GDPR & UK GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we process your information based on your explicit consent under the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF v2.2).

  • Consent Management: You will be presented with a Consent Management Platform (CMP) dialog upon launching the app to manage your preferences.
  • Withdrawing Consent: You can withdraw your consent or modify your ad-tracking preferences at any time by accessing the "Privacy Settings" option within the application's Help or Settings menu.

5. United States Privacy Rights (CCPA, CPRA & State Laws)

Residents of California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, and other applicable US states possess specific privacy rights regarding their data. We operate under an opt-out model for these jurisdictions.

  • Categories Collected: In the past 12 months, we have collected "Identifiers" (IP address, Device/Ad IDs) and "Internet or other similar network activity" (Product Interaction, Advertising Data) for business and advertising purposes.
  • Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information: Sharing your Advertising ID for targeted ads may be considered a "sale" or "sharing" under certain state laws. You may exercise your right to opt-out of targeted advertising at any time by accessing the "Privacy Settings" option located within the application's Help or Settings menu.

6. Other Global Privacy Rights (Canada, Australia, Brazil)

We process personal data in compliance with international laws, including Canada’s PIPEDA, Australia’s Privacy Act 1988, and Brazil’s LGPD. Users in these regions have the right to request access to or correction of their data. As stated in Section 3, since we do not store personal data on our servers, exercising these rights is effectively managed by adjusting your device settings or resetting your Advertising ID.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not intended for or directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please instruct them to uninstall the app and reset the device's Advertising ID to clear any third-party tracking.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technical changes or legal compliance updates. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top.
