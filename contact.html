Connecting a drive

The app says no device is connected

The app can only see a drive that Android itself has already recognised. Work through this in order:

  1. Open your phone's Settings, search for OTG, and switch it on if the option exists.
  2. Check your adapter is a real data OTG adapter, not a charge-only cable. This is by far the most common cause.
  3. Open your phone's own file manager. If the drive isn't visible there either, no app can reach it — the problem is the cable, the port or the drive.
  4. Test the same drive on a PC to rule the drive out.
  5. Check the charging port for dust or lint.
Does my phone support OTG?

Most Android phones made in the last several years do. If searching Settings for OTG returns nothing, your phone most likely enables it automatically — the absence of a toggle isn't a sign it's unsupported. Confirm by plugging in a drive and checking your file manager.

Do I need root?

No — not for USB drives over OTG, which is what these apps are built for.

Root is only needed to reach the phone's internal SD card slot, because Android doesn't expose it the same way. If you need to work on a microSD card without root, put it in a USB card reader and connect that over OTG instead.

Can I use an external hard drive?

Flash drives, card readers, portable SSDs and OTG hubs are supported. Mechanical external hard drives (HDDs) are not officially supported — they draw more power than a phone's USB port reliably provides, so they may fail to spin up or cut out mid-operation.

If you try one anyway: charge the phone fully first, and use a powered USB hub if you have one.

The drive disconnects part-way through an operation

A drive that drops out mid-write is almost always a physical connection problem, not a software one:

  • Don't move the phone or the cable while an operation is running — a fractionally loose adapter will disconnect on the smallest movement.
  • Try a different OTG adapter. Cheap adapters fail under sustained load even when they work for a quick file copy.
  • Keep the phone charged; a drive under sustained write load draws real power.
Android says the drive is corrupted or unsupported after I format it

That notification is expected after formatting to NTFS or ext2/3/4, and it does not mean anything is wrong. Most Android devices have no built-in driver for either, so the system sees a file system it can't read and reports the drive as damaged or unsupported.

The format succeeded and the app can still use the drive normally, because it reads and writes these file systems with its own native drivers rather than relying on Android's.

Dismiss the notification and never tap the option to format — that offer comes from Android, not from us, and it will erase the drive you just prepared.

Ads & tokens

The ad won't load, or says “try again”

This is the single most common report, and it's nearly always the network — not the app. In order of how often it's the fix:

  1. Private DNS. Open Settings, search for Private DNS, choose Private DNS provider hostname and enter dns.google. Save, toggle your connection off and on, then restart the app. Ad-blocking DNS providers block the ad servers, so no ad can load.
  2. Ad blockers and blocking VPNs. Disable them for the app. There is nothing to load through a blocker.
  3. Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data. If ads load on one and not the other, the network is the cause.
  4. Watch the ad to the very end and close it with the X that appears. Pressing Back early cancels the reward.
Why does the app need internet just to format a drive?

The operation itself doesn't. The connection is for loading ads, which is what funds the free version — the ads are the price of the tools being free.

If you want the apps to work fully offline, the one-time Pro upgrade removes ads and the token system entirely, and offline use comes with it.

How do I get tokens?

Two ways: watch a rewarded ad (about a minute), or buy them directly. Watching an ad is free and unlimited over time.

The “watch an ad” option disappeared

That's a cap, not a bug. Earning by ads stops at a maximum balance of 5 tokens. Spend some and the option comes back on its own once you're under the cap.

Which actions cost tokens?

The large majority of tools are free. Tokens apply only to the heavy write operations — formatting, the partition wizard, installing or updating Ventoy, and burning a Windows image. The cost is one to three tokens depending on the operation, and the exact figure is always shown before you confirm.

Wiping, backup and restore, bad-blocks checking, benchmarking, defragmenting, the file manager, image conversion and the console-game tools are all free.

I watched the ad but got no token

The reward is granted when the ad reports completion, so it's lost if the ad is closed early or the connection drops mid-ad. Watch to the end, close with the X, and stay connected.

If the ad definitely completed and the balance didn't move, force-stop the app (Settings → Apps → the app → Force stop) and reopen it while online.

My tokens disappeared

Token balances are stored on your device, not on an account. Clearing the app's data, uninstalling, or moving to a new phone clears them.

Pro is different — it's tied to your Google account and survives all of that.

Formatting & file systems

Which file system should I choose?
  • FAT32 — maximum compatibility (TVs, car stereos, consoles, BIOS boot). Single files are limited to 4 GB.
  • exFAT — no practical file-size limit, still read by phones, Windows and macOS. The best default for general use.
  • NTFS — for Windows. No practical file-size limit, but most Android phones can't read it.
  • ext2/3/4 — for Linux. Neither Android nor Windows reads it without extra software.
I formatted to NTFS (or ext4) and now my phone can't read the drive

The format succeeded. The limitation is Android itself: most Android devices have no built-in NTFS or ext2/3/4 support, so the system can't mount the drive even though a PC will read it perfectly. A handful of manufacturers add NTFS support, but it is the exception rather than the rule.

Inside our apps the drive still works normally — they carry their own native drivers for these file systems instead of relying on Android's.

If you need the drive readable by the phone's own file manager and other apps, use exFAT. It has no practical file-size limit and is read by Android, Windows and macOS alike.

Format failed, or the verification check failed
  1. Confirm the adapter is a data OTG adapter and reseat it.
  2. Test the drive on a PC — a drive that fails verification usually has failing flash.
  3. Try a different USB port on the adapter or a different adapter entirely.
  4. Run the bad-blocks check to confirm whether the flash itself is at fault.

A verification failure means data written to the drive read back differently. That is a hardware symptom, and no app can format around it.

The drive shows less capacity than it should

Two ordinary causes and one bad one:

  • Manufacturers count in decimal gigabytes; your phone counts in binary. A "64 GB" drive genuinely holds about 59 GiB.
  • A previous bootable write may have left hidden partitions taking up space. Use the partition wizard, or wipe the drive and format fresh, to recover it.
  • If the shortfall is large, the drive may be counterfeit — see the drive health section.
How do I get back unallocated space?

Open the partition wizard, select the unallocated block and create a partition, or delete the existing partitions and let the app lay out the full drive. Changes are queued so you can review them, then applied together.

The quickest route, if you don't need what's on the drive: wipe it and format fresh.

MBR or GPT?

GPT for modern UEFI machines. MBR for older BIOS/legacy boot, and for consoles like the PS2 that require it.

You can convert between them in the app, without a PC. Back the drive up first regardless — any change to a partition table is a change you don't want to get halfway through.

Bootable USB

Which app should I use to make a bootable drive?
  • DROFUS — one ISO, maximum control over the result. The straightforward choice for a Windows or Linux installer.
  • Ventoy (Unofficial) — install once, then drag as many ISOs onto the drive as it holds and pick at boot. Best if you carry several installers.
  • Ultimate USB — contains both, plus partitioning, diagnostics and the rest.
The drive was created but the PC won't boot from it

The write can be perfect and the PC still refuse it. Check, in this order:

  1. Boot menu. Actually select the USB drive in the boot menu (usually F12, F11, F9 or Esc at power-on) rather than expecting it to boot on its own.
  2. Secure Boot. Disable it in the BIOS/UEFI, or use a build that supports it.
  3. Partition scheme. GPT drives need UEFI mode; MBR drives need legacy/CSM. A mismatch means the drive simply won't appear.
  4. USB port. Try a rear port directly on the motherboard, not a front-panel or hub port.
  5. The ISO itself. Re-download it if it came from anywhere other than the official source — a truncated ISO writes fine and boots into nothing.
My Windows ISO has a file bigger than 4 GB

That's install.wim, and FAT32 can't hold it. The app splits it automatically into .swm parts so the drive can stay FAT32 — which is what Secure Boot machines usually need. Windows setup reassembles the parts itself; nothing is lost.

The alternative is NTFS, which has no size limit but isn't accepted by every UEFI firmware.

How do I switch Ventoy between MBR and GPT?

Inside the app, before installing — you don't need a PC. Choose the partition table on the main screen, then tap Install.

Install wipes the drive completely, including any ISOs already copied to it, so copy them back afterwards with the copy-to-USB button.

How do I add or remove ISOs on a Ventoy drive?

Once Ventoy is installed, just copy ISO files onto the drive like ordinary files, or download them straight to the drive from inside the app. No reburning, no reinstalling. Deleting an ISO removes it from the boot menu.

What is Linux persistence, and why is the option greyed out?

Persistence adds a separate ext3/ext4 partition so files and changes survive a reboot of the live system.

It's only enabled for ISOs that are confirmed to support it — most distributions don't, and offering it for those would produce a drive that silently fails to keep anything.

What is Windows To Go?

A full Windows installation that runs from the USB drive itself, rather than an installer that puts Windows onto a PC. Use a fast drive — a slow flash drive makes it painful to use.

Can I burn a macOS DMG or a Raspberry Pi image?

Yes — macOS DMG (Intel-based), Raspberry Pi OS and other ARM images are written as raw blocks, preserving the original layout and boot structure. There's no customisation applied to these, by design.

Failures & stalls

The operation is stuck or won't finish

Two causes cover almost every case:

  1. The app was minimised. Operations pause when the app leaves the foreground — that's deliberate, to protect the drive from a half-finished write. Keep it on screen and keep the screen awake.
  2. The adapter or drive. Reseat the adapter, don't move the phone while it runs, and try another adapter if it recurs. A loose or charge-only adapter is the usual culprit.

Large drives and large images simply take time — a write that looks frozen on a slow drive is often still progressing. If the progress figure hasn't moved at all for a long stretch, treat it as a stall and check the two points above.

It stalls near the very end — 99%, or in the last few percent

The end of a write is where data reaches the far end of the drive. Stalling there is the classic signature of a drive that reports more capacity than it physically has, or whose flash is failing at the far end.

Run the bad-blocks check with fake-capacity detection to confirm. If it comes back bad, no app can fix it — the drive is the problem.

Can the app damage my USB drive?

No. Nothing the app does is unrecoverable at the hardware level — the worst case is a drive that needs formatting again. If a drive stopped working after an operation, it was already failing.

Can I use my phone while an operation runs?

Not on other apps — operations pause when the app is minimised. Leave it in the foreground until it finishes. Long operations on large drives can take a while, so start them when you don't need the phone.

The app crashed or closed by itself

First check you installed from Google Play. Modified or repackaged copies from APK sites are a common source of crashes we can't reproduce or fix.

If it's the Play version, write to us with the app version, your phone model, the drive size and file system, and what you were doing when it closed. Crashes we can reproduce get fixed quickly.

Drive health

How do I tell if a drive is counterfeit?

Run the bad-blocks check with fake-capacity detection. It writes across the whole drive and reads it back — a counterfeit drive reports a large capacity but silently discards anything past its real size, which the test exposes.

Symptoms before you test: files that copy successfully but are corrupt afterwards, and writes that stall near the end.

Can a corrupted drive be repaired?

Logical corruption — a broken partition table or file system — is usually fixable by wiping and formatting fresh. That's what the repair tools address.

Physical flash failure isn't repairable by any software. If the bad-blocks check reports errors, replace the drive.

Does secure wipe really erase everything?

It performs a full single-pass zero overwrite of the whole device — equivalent to the "Clear" level in NIST SP 800-88. That defeats all normal file-recovery software.

It is not a multi-pass or "Purge"-level erase, and we don't claim it is. For legally mandated destruction standards, follow your own policy.

My drive is slower than advertised

Run the benchmark for real numbers. Bear in mind the whole chain limits speed: a USB 3.0 drive in a USB 2.0 adapter runs at USB 2.0 speed, and advertised figures are usually peak read, not sustained write.

Small-file writes are always dramatically slower than large sequential ones. That's how flash works, not a fault.

Console games

How do I set up a drive for PS2 / OPL?

Format the drive to FAT32 with an MBR partition table, then use the PS2 tools to import your games. The app handles the USBExtreme (.ul) conversion for images over 4 GB and generates the ul.cfg and playlists.

Run the defragmenter afterwards — OPL requires unfragmented files, and skipping this is the usual cause of games that won't load.

Which game formats can be converted?

Between ISO, BIN/CUE, VCD, ZSO, CSO and CHD, in the directions each format supports. Compressing to ZSO or CSO saves considerable space at a small performance cost.

Do you supply the games, BIOS files, or console firmware?

No. The apps are tools for organising files you already own. We don't bundle, host or distribute game images, BIOS files, console firmware or any proprietary console component, and we won't add features that depend on them.

Game covers aren't downloading

Artwork is fetched online, so it needs a working connection. Games with an unusual or renamed game ID may have no artwork available to match.

Apps & data

It's been retired. Every feature it had is in DROFUS, along with everything added since — install that instead.

Ultimate USB. It contains the bootable-USB writer, Ventoy, formatting, partitioning, diagnostics, backup and the console-game tools in one app. The separate apps exist for people who want just one job done.

Do my files get uploaded anywhere?

No. Every operation runs locally on your device. The contents of your files, images and drives are never uploaded — there is no cloud service behind these apps. The full detail is in the privacy policy.

Do the apps create an account?

No. There's no sign-up, no profile and no account to delete. Pro is tied to your Google Play purchase, which Google manages, and tokens live on your device.

How do I restore a backup, or open a .bk file?

Use Restore to write a backup back to a drive. To inspect one without restoring it, open the .bk file in the partition image manager and browse its contents.

Is the app available in my language?

The apps are translated into many languages and follow your phone's language setting. If a translation is wrong or missing, send us the screen and the correct wording — corrections from native speakers are genuinely welcome.

ChaoMe: my theme resets after a while

MIUI and HyperOS revert themes on their own — the theme file is applied correctly, then the system replaces it. There's no reliable fix from an app without root; it's a limitation of the theme engine, not of the export.

ChaoMe: why does it need Shizuku?

From Android 11 onwards, Google's storage rules stop any app from writing into the Themes app's private folder. Shizuku bridges that gap so the theme installs cleanly — and it means no root is required. The app walks you through the one-time setup.
