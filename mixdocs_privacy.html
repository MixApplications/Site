Privacy Policy — MixDocs

This policy applies to the MixDocs app (documents, PDF and scanner) published by MixApplications. It is specific to MixDocs; other MixApplications apps are covered by the general privacy policy.

Last updated: September 27, 2026
The short version

MixDocs opens, edits, converts, scans and recognises text in your documents entirely on your device. Your files, scans and the text in them are never uploaded, and MixDocs has no account and no cloud. The only information that leaves your device is what the advertising SDKs need to show ads (with your consent where the law requires it), and the purchase record Google Play keeps if you buy MixDocs Pro, which removes all ads.

1. Overview: Your Documents Stay on Your Device

Everything MixDocs does with your documents happens on your phone or tablet: viewing and editing PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint and OpenDocument files, converting, merging, compressing, redacting, signing, scanning paper, and recognising text (OCR). The code that processes documents has no network access. MixApplications never receives your files, your scans, the text inside them, their names or your search terms.

MixDocs has no account, no sign-in and no cloud storage of its own. We do not use analytics or attribution services, and we do not add crash-reporting services.

2. Files, Scans and Permissions

  • Files you open are read through Android’s system file picker or “Open with”; MixDocs sees only the files and folders you choose. Folders you add can be removed at any time in the app.
  • Files MixDocs creates (converted files, copies you save, scans) are written as new files in Download/MixDocs or where you choose. Your original file is never changed unless you explicitly choose “Replace original”.
  • Scanning uses Google’s on-device document scanner (ML Kit); pages are processed on the device.
  • Library data (recent files, thumbnails, reading position, settings) is stored only in the app’s private storage on your device.
  • Permissions: internet and network state (used only by the advertising SDKs and Google Play), notifications, a foreground service and wake lock (progress of long tasks such as converting or recognising text), vibration (touch feedback), Google Play billing (MixDocs Pro) and the advertising ID (ads). MixDocs does not request storage, camera, location, contacts, microphone or phone permissions.

3. How Ads and Consent Work

Ads are provided through the Appodeal mediation platform and the advertising networks reachable through it (listed in Section 13). Before any ad is requested, MixDocs asks Appodeal’s Consent Management Platform together with Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) whether a consent prompt is required for you (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, or under US state laws), and shows it when needed. Your choice is respected by the networks: without consent, only non-personalized ads are requested where the law requires it.

Ads never appear inside an open document, inside a running tool, in Settings or in dialogs. The advertising SDKs process your device’s advertising ID, technical device and network information (including IP address, from which an approximate location may be derived) and ad interactions, under their own privacy policies. They never receive your documents or their contents.

4. MixDocs Pro (Purchase)

MixDocs Pro is a one-time purchase that removes all ads; every tool stays free for everyone. The purchase is processed by Google Play under Google’s terms. MixDocs never sees your payment details; the app only keeps, on your device, whether Pro is active. When Pro is active the advertising SDKs are not started.

5. Optional Downloads

Some parts of MixDocs are delivered by Google Play when you ask for them, such as the optional text-recognition language pack. The download is made by Google Play, like an app update; MixDocs itself does not contact our servers or any other server for it.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Everything MixDocs stores lives on your device. You can clear the app’s cache and recent-files list in the app, remove folders you added, or delete all app data by uninstalling MixDocs or using Android Settings → Apps → MixDocs → Storage → Clear storage. Files you saved are your own files and stay where you saved them.

Data collected by the advertising SDKs is retained by those providers according to their own policies (see Section 13). Because MixApplications does not receive identifiers from them, a deletion request for advertising data is best made directly to the provider; you can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android settings.

7. Your Choices and Controls

  • Ad privacy choices: open Settings → Ad privacy choices in MixDocs at any time to review or change your consent.
  • No ads at all: buy MixDocs Pro.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Notifications: turn them off in Android settings; long tasks still run.

8. European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the EU/EEA or the UK, your personal data is processed in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK GDPR. The legal bases are Consent (for personalized advertising), Legitimate Interests (for delivering non-personalized ads and preventing fraud and invalid traffic) and Contract (to provide MixDocs Pro after a purchase). You have:

  • The right to request access to the personal data that we process.
  • The right to correct inaccurate personal data.
  • The right to request deletion of your personal data.
  • The right to restrict or object to processing, and to data portability.
  • The right to withdraw your consent at any time (Section 7).
  • The right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

9. United States Privacy Rights (CCPA)

This section outlines the rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) for users in California, as well as residents of Virginia (VCDPA), Colorado (CPA), Connecticut (CTDPA), Utah (UCPA) and other states with similar laws.

  • Right to Know About Data Sales: You have the right to know whether your personal data is sold or shared.
  • Right to Know: You have the right to request disclosure of what personal data we collect and share.
  • Right to Delete: You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights; every tool in MixDocs stays available.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale or Sharing: You have the right to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of your personal data for targeted advertising.

How to Opt-Out of the Sale or Sharing of Your Data

  • In-App Control: open Settings → Ad privacy choices in MixDocs and turn off personalized advertising / “Do not sell or share my personal information”.
  • Email Request: send an opt-out request to our Data Protection Officer at islam.saad2005@gmail.com.
  • Device Settings: reset or delete your device’s Advertising ID in Android settings.

10. Brazil Privacy Rights (LGPD)

If you are in Brazil, you have the rights provided by the Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD), including confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymization or deletion, portability, information about sharing, and withdrawal of consent. Contact us as described in Section 15.

11. Other Global Privacy Rights

MixDocs is used worldwide. Wherever you live, you can ask us about the personal data processed through MixDocs, and we will respond within the time your local law requires (for example the privacy laws of Canada, Switzerland, Türkiye, India, South Africa, Japan, South Korea, Australia and the countries of the Middle East and North Africa).

12. Children and Audience

MixDocs is a productivity app for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not ask for a birth date or any identity document. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us (Section 15) and we will address it.

13. Data Types Collected and Shared

To align with our Google Play Data safety declaration, the following data is collected and/or shared only by the third-party SDKs listed below. MixApplications itself does not receive it.

  • Device or other IDs — including the Advertising ID and app set ID.
  • App interactions — ad impressions and clicks, app launches seen by the ad SDKs.
  • Diagnostics — crash and performance data of the ad SDKs themselves.
  • Approximate location — inferred by advertising SDKs from IP address; MixDocs does not request location permissions.
  • Purchase history — only if you buy MixDocs Pro, kept by Google Play.

MixDocs does not collect your files, documents, photos, scans, their text, contacts, messages, precise location, or personal information such as your name or email address.
