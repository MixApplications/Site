Privacy Policy — TumTum

This policy applies to the TumTum game published by MixApplications. It is specific to TumTum; other MixApplications apps are covered by the general privacy policy.

Last updated: September 17, 2026
The short version

TumTum is a single-player sliding puzzle game. It runs on your device, needs no account, and has no in-app purchases. The only information that ever leaves your device is (1) optional crash diagnostics, which you can switch off, and (2) advertising data handled by third-party ad partners after you have been asked for consent where the law requires it.

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game TumTum (the “Game”), published by MixApplications ("we," "us," or "our"), handles information. By downloading or playing the Game, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Overview

TumTum is a single-player puzzle game that runs on your device. It does not ask you to register, and it does not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, files, or precise location. Your game progress (levels completed, stars, and coins) is stored only on your device.

Two limited categories of data leave the device, both described below: (1) optional crash diagnostics, which you can turn off in the Game’s settings (see Sections 4 and 11); and (2) advertising data used by third-party ad partners to show ads, handled with consent where required (see Sections 3 and 11).

2. No In-App Purchases

TumTum contains no in-app purchases and no paid content. Coins are earned only by playing and, optionally, by choosing to watch a rewarded ad. We do not process any payment, and we never collect or store credit-card or financial information. There is no account, no “Pro” entitlement, and no server-side purchase verification.

3. How Ads and Consent Work

Ads are provided through the Appodeal mediation platform and the advertising networks reachable through it (listed in Section 11). Before any ad is requested, the Game asks a Consent Management Platform — Appodeal’s CMP together with Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) — to determine whether a consent prompt is required for you (for example in the EEA and the UK) and to record your choice. If consent is not given, ads are either not shown or shown without personalization. Ad content in TumTum is limited to a general-audience (G) rating.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not maintain a database of user profiles.

  • On-device data (progress, coins, settings) is removed when you uninstall the Game.
  • Crashlytics diagnostic data is retained for up to 90 days and then deleted automatically.
  • Advertising data is retained by each advertising partner under its own policy (see Section 11).

Right to Request Deletion: Uninstalling the app permanently removes all data stored locally on your device, including your coin balance. To request deletion of data tied to your Advertising ID, reset or delete that identifier in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads), or contact us using the details in Section 13.

5. Your Choices and Controls

  • Ad personalization / “Do not sell or share.” In the Game, open Settings → Privacy. There is a switch to turn off ad personalization (labelled “Do not sell or share my personal information” for users in the United States). Turning it off keeps ads showing but stops them being chosen using your Advertising ID. The choice is reversible.
  • Ad and data choices (consent form). Where a privacy prompt is required for your region, Settings → Privacy → Ad and data choices reopens the consent form so you can change your decision.
  • Crash reports. Turn diagnostics on or off in Settings → Crash reports.
  • Device controls. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android settings at any time.

6. European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the EU/EEA or the UK, your personal data is processed in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK GDPR. We process information based on the following legal bases: Consent (for personalized advertising), Legitimate Interests (for diagnosing crashes, preventing fraud and invalid traffic, and delivering non-personalized ads), and compliance with legal obligations. You possess the following rights:

  • The right to request access to the personal data that we process.
  • The right to correct inaccurate personal data.
  • The right to request deletion of your personal data.
  • The right to restrict or object to processing, and to data portability.
  • The right to withdraw your consent at any time.
  • The right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

7. United States Privacy Rights (CCPA)

This section outlines the rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) for users in California, as well as residents of Virginia (VCDPA), Colorado (CPA), Connecticut (CTDPA), and Utah (UCPA).

  • Right to Know About Data Sales: You have the right to know whether your personal data is sold or shared.
  • Right to Know: You have the right to request disclosure of what personal data we collect and share.
  • Right to Delete: You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale or Sharing: You have the right to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of your personal data for targeted advertising.

How to Opt-Out of the Sale or Sharing of Your Data

You can use any of the following methods:

  • In-App Control: Open Settings → Privacy in the Game and turn off ad personalization / “Do not sell or share my personal information.” This is available to all users, not only where a regional prompt is required.
  • Email Request: Send an opt-out request to our Data Protection Officer at islam.saad2005@gmail.com.
  • Device Settings: Reset or delete your device’s Advertising ID from your Android system settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

8. Brazil Privacy Rights (LGPD)

Under the Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD), users in Brazil have the right to request access, correction, anonymization, blocking, or deletion of personal data. You can exercise these rights using the methods described above.

9. Other Global Privacy Rights

We aim to honor data subject rights for users in other jurisdictions with comprehensive privacy laws, including Canada (PIPEDA), South Korea (PIPA), Japan (APPI), South Africa (POPIA), India (DPDPA), and Australia (Privacy Act 1988), to the extent required by applicable law. Where those laws apply to you, you may request access, correction, or deletion using the contact details in Section 13. This section describes rights we extend on request and is not a representation of independently certified or audited compliance with each named framework.

10. Children and Audience

TumTum’s declared target audience on Google Play is users aged 13 and older. The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. For users identified by Google’s signals as being under the age of consent, ad requests are tagged using Google’s industry-standard “under age of consent” mechanism so that ads are non-personalized. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us information, contact us (Section 13) and we will address it.

11. Data Types Collected and Shared

To operate and monetize the Game, we collect and share specific categories of data through third-party SDKs. To align with our Google Play Data Safety declaration, we declare that we collect and/or share the following data types:

  • Diagnostics — crash logs (stack traces, device model and OS version, and the current level, chapter and screen we attach as context; no file contents and no personal identifiers) and other app performance data.
  • App interactions — used by advertising SDKs.
  • Approximate location — inferred by advertising SDKs from network/IP information; the Game does not request or use Android location permissions or APIs directly.
  • Device or other IDs — including the Advertising ID.

The Game does not collect purchase history (there are no purchases) and does not use Firebase Analytics.
