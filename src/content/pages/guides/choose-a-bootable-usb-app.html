2. USB drive safety: checked before, during and after every job

Does it test the drive for fake capacity before writing?

A counterfeit drive reports more space than it has, and a write to it fails or loses data. A safe app checks the drive before it writes.

Ultimate USB, DROFUS and Ventoy (Unofficial) check each drive when you connect it. If reading or writing at the end of the drive fails, the drive may be fake or faulty, and they ask whether to Scan for real size or Continue anyway. After a scan, every tool works inside the real size. No tool refuses such a drive: if you keep the claimed size, every write warns you once before anything is written. For a full test of the whole drive, Ultimate USB has Bad Blocks Check.

Does it refuse a drive that cannot be written?

Yes. In all three apps, a drive that is write-protected or does not take writes is recognised as soon as you start a job, with one clear message, and nothing is charged.

Does it warn before erasing the drive?

Writing a bootable drive erases it, so the app should ask first. Ultimate USB and DROFUS show All Data Will Be Permanently Deleted before ISO Burner and Raw Write start. Ultimate USB and Ventoy (Unofficial) show The disk will be formatted and all data will be lost. before Ventoy Install or Clear. You confirm with OK or stop with Cancel.

Does it stop settings that would not work on the drive?

Yes. On a drive larger than 2 TB, the apps use GPT and do not offer FAT32 or MBR, which cannot handle that size. Ventoy is not installed on a drive with 4096-byte sectors, which Ventoy cannot use, and the app tells you why.

Does it detect a truncated, corrupted or non-bootable image?

An ISO from an interrupted download writes without error but does not boot. In Ultimate USB and DROFUS, ISO Burner warns when an ISO looks truncated or corrupted and when the selected file is not a bootable image. It refuses images that are incomplete or damaged and asks you to download them again.

Does it check the free space first?

In Ultimate USB and DROFUS, ISO Burner checks that the drive is large enough and, in High-speed mode, that the phone has enough free storage before it starts. In Ultimate USB and Ventoy (Unofficial), Copy File To USB and downloads to a Ventoy drive check the free space on the drive and show Not Enough Space. when the file does not fit.

Does it check the result before it reports success?

Yes. In Ultimate USB, DROFUS and Ventoy (Unofficial), every format, Ventoy install and boot setup is tested before success is shown. Image writes wait until the drive confirms the data is written, and a wipe can read the drive back. If a check fails, the app reports the failure instead of a success.

Does it check the file system after formatting?

In Ultimate USB, DROFUS and Ventoy (Unofficial), every job that formats a drive checks and tests the file system afterwards and only then reports success. This includes Format USB, ISO Burner and Ventoy Install. If a check fails, the job is reported as failed.

Does a cancelled job leave a drive that looks good but is not?

No. If you cancel a format, the app makes sure the unfinished drive is not opened by mistake, and a burn that fails or is cancelled leaves the drive marked to be formatted again, so a half-made drive is never mistaken for a good one.

3. Hardware safety and connection recovery: protected when something goes wrong

What happens if the USB connection fails for a moment?

Our USB apps use a very advanced USB connection. If anything goes wrong while data is being transferred, the app recovers the connection. It protects your USB devices from damage and your data from loss, and it tells you when a write did not complete instead of reporting success.

  • Recovers by itself. If the USB connection has a short failure during a job, the app recovers it automatically and the job continues, with no action from you.
  • Stays connected. A connected drive does not drop off when it is idle.

What happens if the drive is unplugged during a job?

The app tells you at once, and your tokens are safe. In all three apps, a job only ever writes to the drive it started on: if a different drive is plugged in, the app never writes to it.

  • Continue after a re-plug. If the drive is unplugged or stops answering during a burn, a backup or a restore, the app shows Device Removed with Retry. Plug the same drive back in and tap Retry: most jobs continue where they stopped. A different drive is never written. If you cancel instead, the tokens come back.
  • Wipe: the wipe is saved and continues from the same pass when the same drive is plugged in again.
  • Recovery scan: the scan pauses and continues when the same drive comes back, in any USB port, and another drive is never read.

Is a running job safe if you leave the app?

Yes. When the app goes to the background, a write, wipe or scan pauses and continues when you come back. The screen stays on whenever the app is open, so a job is not cut off.

Does it give tokens back when a write does not complete?

If an app charges for a write, a failed write should not cost you. In Ultimate USB, DROFUS and Ventoy (Unofficial), the tokens come back when a paid job fails, when a burn or a format is cancelled, and when nothing was written. If the app is closed during a job, the tokens come back the next time it opens.

4. What the apps can do: advanced options for every job

Does it choose the right settings for you?

Yes. In Ultimate USB and DROFUS, ISO Burner sets the partition table to suit the image, offers only the file systems that fit the image and the drive, and for Windows To Go selects the Pro edition first. If the BIOS boot loader cannot be installed for a Linux image, the drive is finished for UEFI computers with a warning instead of failing. MS-DOS and FreeDOS drives are made without any image.

Does it offer GPT or MBR, and NTFS, FAT32 or exFAT?

The partition table must match how the PC starts: GPT for UEFI, MBR for legacy BIOS or CSM.

  • Ultimate USB and DROFUS (ISO Burner): GPT (UEFI) or MBR (BIOS or UEFI-CSM); FAT32 or NTFS for bootable images, and FAT32, exFAT or NTFS for a Non-bootable drive.
  • Ultimate USB and Ventoy (Unofficial) (Ventoy): MBR or GPT, and FAT32, exFAT or NTFS.

Can it write compressed images directly?

Yes. Ultimate USB and DROFUS write disk images packed in .gz, .xz, .bz2 or .zip and unpack them as they write, so you do not need to unpack them first. The archive must hold one bootable disk image.

Can it split install.wim for FAT32?

Many PCs with Secure Boot need a FAT32 drive, but FAT32 cannot hold a file over 4 GB. Ultimate USB and DROFUS offer Split image for FAT32 compatibility., which splits install.wim into parts. Windows setup puts the parts back together.

Can it set Windows up for you before it installs?

Yes. In Ultimate USB and DROFUS, Windows Setup Customization can remove the 4 GB RAM, Secure Boot and TPM 2.0 requirement, remove the online Microsoft account requirement, create a local account with your user name, skip the privacy questions, turn off BitLocker automatic encryption, stop Windows forcing Copilot, OneDrive and Outlook, and use your phone's regional settings.

Can you pick the exact Windows edition?

In Ultimate USB and DROFUS, when you write Windows To Go from an image with more than one edition, the app lists the editions and you choose one.

Does Windows To Go protect the PC's own drives?

Yes. In Ultimate USB and DROFUS, Set the target's internal drives offline is on by default for Windows To Go, so the portable Windows does not touch the drives inside the PC.

Is it ready for the new Secure Boot certificates?

Yes. For recent Windows 11 images, Ultimate USB and DROFUS can use "Windows CA 2023" signed bootloaders for PCs that support them, and can apply SkuSiPolicy.p7b (KB5042562) on installation.

Can it keep your Linux changes between boots?

Yes, in two ways. ISO Burner in Ultimate USB and DROFUS adds a persistent partition (ext3 or ext4) with the size you choose. The Ventoy persistence wizard in Ultimate USB and Ventoy (Unofficial) creates a persistence image from 256 MB to 128 GB and picks the right label for Ubuntu, Linux Mint, MX Linux, Debian, Kali, Arch Linux, Manjaro or Fedora.

Can you pick the Ventoy version?

In Ultimate USB and Ventoy (Unofficial), the Ventoy version sheet lists the built-in version, versions you downloaded, and Other version…, which lists more Ventoy releases. The version you select is used for Install and Update.

Can you change Ventoy's settings without a PC?

Yes. Config Hub in Ultimate USB and Ventoy (Unofficial) edits ventoy.json with no typing: more than 30 settings, including menu timeout, default entry, menu language, keyboard layout, Windows 11 bypasses, renamed menu entries, tips and a boot password. Settings it does not show are kept, and a damaged ventoy.json is repaired with a backup. You can also download ISO files straight to the drive, and update Ventoy without losing them.

Does it give you proof that a drive was wiped?

Yes. Wipe USB in Ultimate USB is free and offers NIST SP 800-88 Clear, Australian ISM, DoD 5220.22-M, Schneier and Gutmann. It can read the drive back to verify, and it makes a certificate of erasure and a full report as a PDF, with the drive's details, the method and the result. On a drive that stores less than it claims, the report says so.

Can it recover files from a failing drive?

Yes. Recover deleted files in Ultimate USB is free, only reads the drive, and finds more than 200 file types, with previews and a chance-of-recovery label for each file. For a drive with read errors, it can copy the whole drive to the phone first and scan the copy. Recover lost partitions puts a lost partition back and saves the old partition table first, so you can undo it.

Can it encrypt a drive?

Yes. USB Encryption in Ultimate USB locks a drive with a password, locks it again when you leave the app, and changes the password quickly without encrypting the files again.

Can it test a drive without risking your files?

Yes. In Ultimate USB, Benchmark in Read / Write mode leaves your files as they were, and Device Info checks the partition table for overlaps, alignment and type mismatches.

Which drives and languages does it support?

Flash drives, portable SSDs, card readers and OTG hubs, with FAT16, FAT32, exFAT, NTFS and EXT2/3/4, without root, on Android 7.0 and later. The apps are available in 58 languages.
