What happens if the USB connection fails or the drive is unplugged?

Our USB apps use a very advanced USB connection. If anything goes wrong while data is being transferred, the app recovers the connection. It protects your USB devices from damage and your data from loss, and it tells you when a write did not complete instead of reporting success.

  • Recovers by itself. If the USB connection has a short failure during a job, the app recovers it automatically and the job continues, with no action from you.
  • Stays connected. A connected drive does not drop off when it is idle.
  • Continue after a re-plug. If the drive is unplugged or stops answering during a burn, a backup or a restore, the app shows Device Removed with Retry. Plug the same drive back in and tap Retry: most jobs continue where they stopped. A different drive is never written. If you cancel instead, the tokens come back.
  • Fake drives are caught. If a drive does not keep the data written to it, the app tells you, keeps its files read-only so nothing more is lost, and asks before any whole-drive tool runs on it.
  • No half-written drives. A burn that fails or is cancelled leaves the drive marked to be formatted again, so a half-written drive is never mistaken for a good one.

We fully test this connection in the worst cases before every release, to make sure it is the best, most stable and safest USB connection.

What does the app check before it writes anything?

  • Drive test on connect. The app checks the drive when you connect it. A drive that fails this check may be counterfeit (smaller than it claims) or faulty, and the app warns you before you use it.
  • Write-protected drives are stopped first. A drive that is write-protected or does not take writes is recognised as soon as you start a job, with one clear message, and nothing is charged.
  • Nothing is erased without your OK. Before a format, a burn or a wipe, the app shows All Data Will Be Permanently Deleted and waits for you to confirm.
  • The image is checked before the drive is wiped. When you burn an image, the app first checks it. An incomplete or unreadable image is refused, and nothing is written to the drive.
  • Warnings about the file. Ultimate USB and DROFUS warn when an ISO looks truncated or corrupted from an interrupted download, and when the file you picked is not bootable.
  • Space is checked. Ultimate USB and DROFUS check the free space on both the drive and the phone before a burn starts.
  • Settings that cannot work are blocked. On a drive larger than 2 TB, the apps use GPT and do not offer FAT32 or MBR. Ventoy is not installed on a drive with 4096-byte sectors, which it cannot use, and the app says why.

How is the drive protected while a job runs?

  • Pause and continue. When the app goes to the background, a write, wipe or scan pauses, and it continues when you come back while the app is still running.
  • The right drive only. In every app, a job only writes to the drive it started on. If a different drive is plugged in, the app never writes to it.
  • Wipes and scans survive a re-plug. A wipe is saved and continues from the same pass, and a recovery scan continues when the same drive comes back, in any USB port. Another drive is never touched.
  • The screen stays on. The screen stays on whenever the app is open, so a job is not cut off.
  • A cancelled format is cleaned up. If you cancel a format, the app makes sure the unfinished drive is not opened by mistake. Format the drive again to use it.
  • Only the partition you picked. Partition Wizard changes only the partition you chose, and you can undo your changes before you apply them.
  • Unsafe drives are not defragmented. USB Defragmenter refuses a drive that was not closed properly, that cannot be read, that is smaller than reported or too full, and says why.
  • Tests that leave your files alone. Benchmark in Read / Write mode leaves your files as they were.
  • Recovery only reads. Recover deleted files never writes to the drive it scans, and Recover lost partitions saves the old partition table first, so you can undo.
  • Encrypted drives lock themselves. In Ultimate USB, an encrypted drive locks when you leave the app.

Does the app check the result before it reports success?

  • Every format is tested. After any format, including a burn or a Ventoy install, the app checks and tests the new file system before it reports success. Image writes wait until the drive confirms the data is written.
  • Proof of erasure. After a wipe, Ultimate USB and USB Tools can read the drive back to verify it, and make a certificate of erasure and a full PDF report on your phone.

Do I lose tokens when a job fails?

No. The app shows the token cost before a job starts. If the job fails, is cancelled, or the drive is removed, the tokens come back automatically (in Partition Wizard, for the steps that did not finish). If the app is closed during a job, they are returned the next time the app starts.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Your files and images are processed on your phone and are never uploaded, and there is no account or sign-up.

  • All the work happens on your phone; no PC is needed.
  • USB drives work without root. NTFS and ext2/3/4 drives that your phone cannot read still open in the app.
  • Nothing the apps do is unrecoverable at the hardware level: the worst case is a drive that needs formatting again.
