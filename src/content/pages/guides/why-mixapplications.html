Are the apps safe for my phone, my drives and my files?

Yes. Every app does its work on your phone, and your files are never uploaded. Our USB apps add safety for your drives at every step:

  • Each drive is tested when you connect it. Read-only and fake drives are caught, and nothing is erased without your OK.
  • Every format is tested before success is shown, and image writes wait until the drive confirms the data is written.
  • A short USB connection failure is recovered by itself and the job continues. Re-plug the same drive and tap Retry to go on.
  • A job only writes to the drive it started on, and your tokens come back when a job does not complete.
  • Every version of our USB apps is fully tested before each release.

Read every check in How our USB apps keep your drive and your data safe.
