How do I start?

MixDocs has the tabs Files, Scan and Tools. Files shows your recent documents: tap one to open it, or long-press to select several. Add a folder to show its files.

To create a new file, tap + at the top of Files and choose a Word document, a sheet, slides, a blank PDF or a template.

Is MixDocs free?

Yes. Every tool and feature is free, with no page limits, no watermarks and no account.

The free version shows ads: banners and cards on its screens, a banner under the document you read, and full-screen ads at breaks, such as after a tool finishes. Ads never cover a document's pages and never appear in an editor or a dialog.

Which files can MixDocs open?

PDF (also password-protected files and forms), Word (DOCX, DOC, ODT, RTF), Excel (XLSX, XLSB, XLS, ODS, CSV, TSV), PowerPoint (PPTX, PPT, ODP), Pages, Numbers, Keynote, Publisher, Visio, Hangul (HWP, HWPX), StarOffice/OpenOffice 1.x (SXW, SXC, SXI, SXD), Lotus 1-2-3, e-books and comics (EPUB, MOBI, AZW, AZW3 without DRM, FB2, CHM, CBZ, CBR), e-mail (EML, MSG), XPS, text, Markdown, HTML, source code and images: more than 50 file types. Office files open in their original layout.

How do I scan paper?

Open the Scan tab and start a scan. The edges are found for you. Choose full, basic or ID card mode, then rotate, crop, recolour, reorder or delete pages before you save. Scans are saved as a PDF in Download/MixDocs.

Where are my files saved?

New files go to Download/MixDocs. Use Save as… on any result to choose another place, or change the folder in Settings → Where new files go. After a reinstall, choose Download/MixDocs there to list your older files again.

Does a tool change my original file?

No. Your original stays as it is, and the result is saved as a new file. The original changes only if you choose Replace original.

Can I edit PDFs and Office files?

Yes. In a PDF, long-press words to edit the text, and add or change pictures and objects from More. The mark-up tools highlight, underline, draw, and add notes and text. You can fill forms and sign by hand or by typing.

Word, OpenDocument and RTF documents, spreadsheets and presentations can be edited too.

Does MixDocs work offline?

Files open and can be read offline and with an ad blocker. Tools, converters and Scan are free thanks to ads, so in the free version they need the internet with ads allowed. With Pro, every tool works offline with no ads. Buying Pro needs a connection once.

A translation, handwriting or spell-check language needs a connection once, when it is downloaded. Background removal also works offline, with a model built into the app.

Why does it ask me to turn off an ad blocker?

An ad blocker is stopping the ads that keep the tools free. Turn off the ad-blocking app, VPN or Private DNS (Android Settings → Network & internet → Private DNS → Automatic or Off), then tap I've allowed ads — check again. Pro removes ads and this check.

Are my documents uploaded?

No. Your documents are opened and changed on your phone; they are never uploaded. There is no account and no cloud. Private mode keeps no recent list, reading positions or search index, and blocks screenshots.

Can I protect a PDF with a password or remove text for good?

Yes. You can add passwords to PDF and Office files. Redaction removes the text for good and checks the result, and you can remove hidden data from a file. You can also add or check digital signatures in a PDF.

Does text recognition or translation need the internet?

Partly. Text recognition runs on the phone with more than 20 scripts built in. Translation downloads a language once on first use. The English, Spanish and French dictionaries are installed with the app and work offline; spell-check dictionaries download once per language when you turn one on, then work offline.

What does Pro give, and how do I restore it?

Pro is a one-time purchase that removes all ads and lets every tool work offline and with an ad blocker. Every tool and feature is free for everyone, with no limits and no watermarks; Pro does not unlock tools.

Pro is tied to your Google Play account. You can also use Settings → MixDocs Pro → Restore. If a payment is pending, Pro turns on by itself once it goes through.
