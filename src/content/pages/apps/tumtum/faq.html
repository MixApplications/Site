How do I play?

Swipe on the board. One swipe moves every character at the same time, and each one slides until it hits a wall, another character or its own door. Get them all home.

The first levels teach the rule. Later boards add pits, spikes, sliding crates, breaking blocks, one-way tiles, springs and moving belts.

Is there a time limit or lives?

No timer and no lives. Each level has a move limit: when you run out, the game shows Out of moves. The limit is based on the shortest solution, and the game tells you how many moves it takes and how many you have.

What happens when a character falls?

Pits and spikes are one-way: only Undo brings a character back. When one falls, the game shows One fell!. Tap Undo a move or Start over.

A level seems impossible. What can I do?

Every level has a solution. The order of your swipes matters. Use Undo or Restart as often as you like, or tap Hint.

How do hints and coins work?

A hint costs 10 coins, and a new game starts with 10 coins. You earn coins by solving levels for the first time (more for more stars), from a bonus for 3-star streaks, and from a Chapter chest every fifth chapter. Replays pay nothing.

If you tap Hint without enough coins and a video is ready, you can tap Watch an ad to earn 15 coins. Watching is always optional.

How do I get stars?

Each level gives 1 to 3 stars, based on how many moves you used compared with the shortest solution.

What does Learn do?

Learn explains each element on the current board, page by page. It is there in chapters 1 to 28, where the elements are introduced.

Is TumTum free? Are there in-app purchases?

TumTum is free and supported by ads. It has no in-app purchases and no Pro version. Every level unlocks by playing. There is no account and no sign-up.

Does TumTum work offline?

Yes. Every level plays fully offline. Ads and the optional videos for extra coins need a connection.

Is my progress kept on a new phone?

Progress, stars and coins are saved on your device. There is no account and no cloud save, so clearing the game's data or uninstalling it resets your progress.

If Android backup is on, reinstalling on the same phone model may bring the progress back. On a different phone model the game starts fresh. Ad consent is asked again after a restore.

Can I turn off music or sounds, or change the language?

Open Settings → Sound & vibration. Sound, music and vibration each have a switch, and sound and music have a volume slider.

TumTum is available in 11 languages and follows your phone's language. To choose another, open Settings → Language; pick System default to follow the phone again. The game works in portrait and landscape.
