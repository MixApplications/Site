Tokens, ads and Pro

How many tokens does a write or a format use, and how do I earn one?

The app shows the number of tokens before you confirm:

  • Install: 2 tokens.
  • Update: 1 token.
  • Clear: 1 token.

Free: Copy File To USB, downloading files to the drive, USB File Manager, Theme Manager, Config Hub and persistence images.

To earn a token, tap the token counter on the main screen to open Get Tokens, then tap Watch under Get 1 free token. One short rewarded video (60 seconds at most) gives 1 token. Free tokens stop at a balance of 5, and at 4 videos each time you open the app. You can also buy tokens. With Pro, no action uses tokens. More: Ventoy tokens.

What happens to my tokens when a write does not complete?

They come back automatically. Tokens are taken when you tap OK, before the job starts. If Install, Update or Clear fails, or nothing was written, the app gives them back.

If the app is closed during a job, the tokens come back the next time you open the app. Install, Update and Clear have no Cancel button.

Why does the rewarded ad not load, and what can I do to still earn a token?

This is nearly always the network. Try these in order:

  1. Private DNS: open Settings, search for Private DNS, choose Private DNS provider hostname and enter dns.google. Save, switch the connection off and on, then restart the app.
  2. Turn off ad blockers and blocking VPNs for the app.
  3. Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data.
  4. Watch the ad to the end and close it with the X. Pressing Back early cancels the reward.

If the ad finished and the balance did not change, force-stop the app (Settings → Apps → the app → Force stop) and open it again while online. You can also buy tokens, or get Pro, which turns the token system off.

Why does the app say an ad blocker or private DNS is active?

The free version cannot load its ads through an ad blocker, a blocking VPN or ad-blocking Private DNS. Running operations wait until it is switched off for the app. Set Private DNS to dns.google or turn it off, then restart the app.

Pro, or any token pack you bought on this install, lets the app run with an ad blocker on.

Why does the app need an internet connection?

Installing Ventoy does not need it. The free version needs a connection to load ads, which pay for the free tools. While you are offline it shows No Internet Connection and running operations wait. They continue on their own when you are back online.

Pro works offline. Downloading files to the drive and loading other Ventoy versions need the internet in any case.

Can I pay instead of watching ads, and what does a purchase include?

Yes. You can buy tokens, or buy Pro. Pro is a one-time, lifetime purchase with no subscription. It removes all ads, turns the token system off, and lets you use the app offline and with an ad blocker on. The Ventoy features are the same with and without Pro.

If a purchase cannot be checked, the app shows Could not verify the purchase. Try again later.

Connecting a drive

Why is my USB drive not detected (USB-C drive, card reader, OTG adapter)?

The phone has to detect the drive first. Work through this list:

  1. Open the phone's Settings, search for OTG and switch it on if the option exists.
  2. Use a data OTG adapter. Charge-only cables cannot carry data.
  3. When Android asks whether the app may use the USB device, tap OK. If you refused, unplug the drive and plug it in again. Check that the mode on the main screen is USB OTG, not SD Slot.
  4. Plug in a FAT32 or exFAT drive and check that the phone notices it. If even that drive is not noticed, the cause is the cable, the port or the drive.
  5. Test the drive on a PC, and check the phone's port for dust.

If the phone does not recognise the drive after Ventoy was installed, it is likely working: test it on a PC.

If a card reader has no card in it, the app says that there is no card or disk in the drive.

Do I need root?

No, not for USB drives over OTG. Root is needed only for the phone's internal SD card slot. Without root, put the card in a USB card reader and connect it over OTG.

Can I use an NTFS drive on Android?

Yes. The app's USB File Manager opens FAT, exFAT, NTFS and EXT drives, also when the phone itself cannot read them. NTFS is the default file system for a Ventoy drive.

Android may say the drive is damaged or unsupported. That is expected: many phones cannot read NTFS on their own. Dismiss the notice and do not tap Android's offer to format: it would erase the drive.

Why does Ventoy refuse my drive?

Ventoy supports only drives with 512-byte sectors. A drive with 4096-byte sectors (4K native) is refused with a message that says so.

The app also checks the drive when you connect it. If reading or writing at the end of the drive fails, the drive may be faulty or counterfeit (smaller than it claims), and the app asks whether to Scan for real size or Continue anyway. The scan finds the size the drive really stores; tap Yes to use it. If you keep the claimed size, Install and Clear warn you once, with Continue and Cancel, before anything is written and before any token is used.

Sometimes the drive fails this check. The app then says so, opens the drive's files read-only and offers Try restore again. Install and Clear still run after one more Yes; No gives the tokens back.

Ventoy drives

How do I put several ISO files on one USB drive?

Install Ventoy once, then copy ISO, WIM, IMG, VHD or EFI files to the drive. When the PC starts, you pick one from the Ventoy menu.

  1. Connect the drive. The Ventoy screen opens when you start the app.
  2. Choose *Partition Table:, *File System: and the other options, and tap Install.
  3. The app shows The disk will be formatted and all data will be lost. and the tokens. Tap OK.
  4. When it is done, copy your ISO files to the drive with Copy File To USB.

More: Ventoy.

Can I install Ventoy with GPT, or only MBR?

Both. Choose MBR or GPT under *Partition Table:, then tap Install. MBR is the default; on a drive over 2 TB, GPT is set and locked.

Install erases the drive, including ISO files already on it, so copy them back afterwards. Update keeps your ISO files and keeps the drive's partition style.

How do I copy ISO files onto a Ventoy drive?

Tap Copy File To USB and pick the file with the system file picker, or tap Download File Directly To USB FROM INTERNET to save a download straight to the drive. Both are free. Copying the files on a PC works too.

The app checks the free space first and shows Not Enough Space. when the file does not fit. When a file with the same name exists, it asks File already exists. Replace it? To remove an ISO, delete it in USB File Manager.

Can I select many files at once?

Yes. In USB File Manager you can pick several files to import at once. A file that cannot be read is skipped, and the rest are copied.

Why does the PC say no bootable file was found on the Ventoy drive?
  • Check that Ventoy In Device: shows a version. If it is empty, Ventoy is not on the drive.
  • Check that the file is one Ventoy can boot: ISO, WIM, IMG, VHD or EFI.
  • Download the file again if the copy may be incomplete. A truncated ISO copies without error but does not boot.
  • In Config Hub → Boot, the sections Finding images (Search root, Max search depth) and Hide from the menu (for example Hide .iso files in menu) change which files the menu lists. Check them if a file is missing.
How do I choose which ISO boots at startup?

When the PC starts from the drive, Ventoy shows a menu of the files on it, and you pick one.

To set a default, open Config Hub → Boot and set Default boot entry and Menu timeout (whole seconds, 0 to 3600). Default menu mode switches between List and TreeView. After you save, the app shows Saved. Takes effect on next boot.

Can I use a specific Ventoy version?

Yes. One Ventoy version is built into the app. Tap the version next to Ventoy In Package: to open the Ventoy version sheet.

  • The built-in version is selected by default.
  • Other version… shows the list of Ventoy releases. Tap a version and confirm the download.
  • Downloaded versions show On this phone and can be deleted.

The version you select is used for both Install and Update. If a downloaded version cannot be used, nothing is written to the drive and the tokens come back.

Can I theme or password-protect a Ventoy drive?

Yes. Theme Manager imports a GRUB2 theme archive (zip, tar.gz or tar.xz) onto the drive. Config Hub sets a boot-menu password, menu options and persistence in the drive's ventoy.json file. Both are free, and the settings are written only to the drive.

Windows on a Ventoy drive

How do I make a bootable Windows USB from my phone?

Install Ventoy on the drive, then copy the Windows ISO to it with Copy File To USB. When the PC starts from the drive, pick the ISO in the Ventoy menu.

The ISO is copied as one file, and FAT32 cannot hold a single file over 4 GB. For an ISO over 4 GB, use exFAT or NTFS for the drive.

How do I install Windows 11 on a PC without TPM 2.0 or Secure Boot?

Open Config Hub → Boot and turn on Windows 11: bypass hardware checks. Windows 11: bypass online account removes the online-account requirement. Save; the change takes effect on the next boot.

Can I copy an ISO with a file over 4 GB to a FAT32 Ventoy drive?

No. FAT32 cannot hold a single file over 4 GB, and on a Ventoy drive the ISO is copied as one file. Use exFAT or NTFS for the drive. The app checks the free space before it copies.

Writing, speed and safety

How long does a write take, and why can it be slow?

The speed depends on the whole chain: a USB 3.0 drive in a USB 2.0 adapter runs at USB 2.0 speed, and many small files write much slower than one large file. Large drives and large ISO files take time.

Why did the write stop at a percentage or the app close during the write?
  • The app was minimised. A job pauses while the app is in the background and continues when it is in front again.
  • No internet or an ad blocker (free version). Running jobs wait until this is fixed. Pro does not pause for these.
  • The drive was removed or stopped answering. The job ends as failed. Reconnect the drive and start again; the tokens come back.
  • A short connection failure. It does not stop the job: the app tries again and goes on.

A stop near the very end often means the drive reports more space than it has, or its flash fails at the end. Reconnect the drive: if the end of the drive fails the check, the app asks whether to Scan for real size or Continue anyway.

If the app was closed during a job, the tokens come back the next time you open it.

Can I use other apps while a write is running?

No. Jobs pause when the app leaves the foreground, to protect the drive. Keep the app open and in front until the job finishes. The app keeps the screen on while it is shown.

My PC does not boot from the USB drive. What should I check?
  1. Boot menu: select the USB drive in the PC's boot menu (often F12, F11, F9 or Esc at power-on).
  2. Secure Boot: keep Secure Boot Support ticked when you install (it is on by default), or turn Secure Boot off in the BIOS/UEFI.
  3. Partition scheme: GPT drives need UEFI mode; MBR drives need legacy or CSM mode.
  4. USB port: try a port directly on the motherboard, not on the front panel or a hub.
  5. The ISO: download it again from the official source. A truncated ISO copies without error but does not boot.
My drive shows less space after writing. How do I get the full capacity back?

Tap Clear to remove Ventoy from the drive (1 token). It formats the drive, so copy off your files first.

Drive makers count in decimal gigabytes and phones in binary ones, so a "64 GB" drive holds about 59 GiB. A large shortfall can mean a counterfeit drive.

Can the app damage my USB drive?

No. Nothing the app does is unrecoverable at the hardware level; the worst case is a drive that needs formatting again. If a drive stopped working after an operation, it was already failing.

The app tests each drive when it is connected, refuses drives Ventoy does not support, and asks for confirmation before Install or Clear erases a drive. Update does not format the drive.

Install checks and tests the file system before the app reports success, and the app reads the drive and shows the Ventoy version now on it under Ventoy In Device:. Every format is tested before success is shown, image writes wait until the drive confirms the data is written, and a job only ever writes to the drive it started on.

A drive that is write-protected, or one that does not take writes, is refused at the start with one message, before any question and before any token is used.

Is the app safe, and is my data uploaded anywhere?

Your files are processed on your phone, and their contents are never uploaded. There is no account and no sign-up. Config Hub and theme settings are written only to the drive. What the app does send, such as ad requests, crash reports and purchase checks, is listed in its privacy policy.

The app asks for Bluetooth permission only on Android 8.1 and older.

Which languages is the app available in?

58 languages. The most recently added are Azerbaijani, Catalan, Estonian, Kazakh, Lithuanian, Latvian, Malayalam, Slovak, Slovenian, Swahili, Tagalog (Filipino) and Uzbek.

On Android 13 and later, you can choose the app's language in the phone's settings.
