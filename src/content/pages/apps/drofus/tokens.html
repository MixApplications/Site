How do I earn a token?

Watch one short rewarded video ad. Each ad gives you one free token. You can also buy a pack of tokens.

If you do not have enough tokens, the app shows Not Enough Tokens with the cost and your balance, and nothing starts. Tap Get Tokens to get more.

Ads need an internet connection.

When are tokens taken?

Only when you tap OK. After you tap START, the app shows All Data Will Be Permanently Deleted with Tokens required: and the number. Tap OK to start, or Cancel to keep your tokens.

What happens to my tokens when a burn does not complete?

They come back to you automatically:

  • when the burn fails or ends with an error;
  • when you cancel the burn;
  • when you answer No to the question about overwriting the whole drive;
  • when the drive is unplugged or stops answering and you cancel the burn (the app first shows Device Removed with Retry: plug the same drive back in and tap Retry to continue from where it stopped);
  • when the app is closed during the burn: the tokens are returned the next time the app starts.

What changes with Pro?

Pro is a one-time purchase. With Pro:

  • no burn uses tokens;
  • there are no ads;
  • the app works offline and with an ad blocker turned on.

Without Pro, the app needs the internet for ads. Ad blockers and blocking Private DNS must be off, unless you bought a token pack on this install. If the connection drops or an ad blocker is found while a burn runs, the burn pauses.

Is the token system fair?

Yes. You can earn tokens for free: watch one short ad and you get a token. Watching a short ad is a small price for the quality and the experience the app gives you.

Tokens are used only for the actions listed on this page. Everything else in the app is free, and the free version simply shows ads.

Buying tokens or Pro is completely optional: it is a way to support the developers and the app. No tool is locked behind a payment, and every tool can be used for free.
