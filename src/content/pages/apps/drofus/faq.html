Tokens, ads and Pro

How many tokens does a write or a format use, and how do I earn one?

The app shows the number of tokens before you confirm:

  • A Windows installation or Windows To Go: 2 tokens.
  • A Linux ISO with Advanced Options (Filesystem, Partition & Persistence) ticked: 2 tokens.
  • MS-DOS, FreeDOS and Non-bootable (format only): 1 token.
  • An image written as it is (Linux, Raspberry Pi, DMG and other .img files): free.

To earn a token, tap the token counter on the main screen to open Get Tokens, then tap Watch under Get 1 free token. One short rewarded video (60 seconds at most) gives 1 token. Free tokens stop at a balance of 5, and at 4 videos each time you open the app. You can also buy a pack. With Pro, no write uses tokens. More: DROFUS tokens.

What happens to my tokens when a write does not complete?

They come back automatically. Tokens are taken when you tap OK, before the burn starts. If the burn fails or you cancel it, or you answer No to the question about overwriting the whole drive, the app gives them back.

If the app is closed during a burn, the tokens come back the next time you open the app.

Why does the rewarded ad not load, and what can I do to still earn a token?

This is nearly always the network. Try these in order:

  1. Private DNS: open Settings, search for Private DNS, choose Private DNS provider hostname and enter dns.google. Save, switch the connection off and on, then restart the app.
  2. Turn off ad blockers and blocking VPNs for the app.
  3. Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data.
  4. Watch the ad to the end and close it with the X. Pressing Back early cancels the reward.

If the ad finished and the balance did not change, force-stop the app (Settings → Apps → the app → Force stop) and open it again while online. You can also buy tokens, or get Pro, which turns the token system off.

Why does the app say an ad blocker or private DNS is active?

The free version cannot load its ads through an ad blocker, a blocking VPN or ad-blocking Private DNS. Running operations wait until it is switched off for the app. Set Private DNS to dns.google or turn it off, then restart the app.

Pro, or any token pack you bought on this install, lets the app run with an ad blocker on.

Why does the app need an internet connection?

Burning does not need it. The free version needs a connection for its banner and rewarded video ads, which keep the core features free. While you are offline it shows No Internet Connection and running operations wait. They continue on their own when you are back online.

Pro works offline.

Can I pay instead of watching ads, and what does a purchase include?

Yes. You can buy tokens, or buy Pro. Pro is a one-time, lifetime purchase with no subscription. It removes all ads, turns the token system off, and lets you use the app offline or with an ad blocker on. The burning features are the same with and without Pro.

If a purchase cannot be checked, the app shows Could not verify the purchase. Try again later.

Connecting a drive

Why is my USB drive not detected (USB-C drive, card reader, OTG adapter)?

The phone has to detect the drive first. Work through this list:

  1. Open the phone's Settings, search for OTG and switch it on if the option exists. The app has a built-in guide for this.
  2. Use a data OTG adapter. Charge-only cables cannot carry data.
  3. When Android asks whether the app may use the USB device, tap OK. If you refused, unplug the drive and plug it in again. Check that the mode on the main screen is USB OTG, not SD Slot.
  4. Plug in a FAT32 or exFAT drive and check that the phone notices it. If even that drive is not noticed, the cause is the cable, the port or the drive.
  5. Test the drive on a PC, and check the phone's port for dust.

DROFUS works with USB flash drives, SD card adapters and external SSDs.

If a card reader has no card in it, the app says that there is no card or disk in the drive.

Do I need root?

No, not for USB drives over OTG. Root is needed only for the phone's internal SD card slot. Without root, put the card in a USB card reader and connect it over OTG.

Why does Android say my NTFS drive is damaged or unsupported?

After a drive is written as NTFS, or after a bootable drive is written, Android may say the drive is damaged or unsupported. That is expected: many phones cannot read NTFS on their own. The write succeeded.

Dismiss the notice and do not tap Android's offer to format: it would erase the drive. To read and write NTFS drives on the phone, you can use our USB File Manager app.

What does "CRITICAL WARNING: BAD DRIVE!" mean?

DROFUS checks the drive when you connect it. If the check fails, the drive may be faulty or counterfeit (smaller than it claims), or the cable may be loose. The app shows 🛑 CRITICAL WARNING: BAD DRIVE! and offers to scan for the size the drive really holds.

Tap Yes to use only the part that works, or try another cable or drive. The app does not burn to a drive that fails this test until the real size has been scanned.

Sometimes the drive fails this check. The app then says so and offers Try restore again. A burn still runs after one more Yes; No gives the tokens back.

Windows

How do I make a Windows 10 or Windows 11 installer, and can I choose the edition?

The burn screen opens when you start the app:

  1. Keep Boot Selection on Disk or ISO image.
  2. Tap Pick ISO and choose the Windows ISO. The app reads the file.
  3. If the app shows the Windows type, keep Standard Windows Installation. Choose the Partition Table and File System, and set the Windows Setup Customization options you want.
  4. Tap START. The app shows All Data Will Be Permanently Deleted and the tokens it uses. Tap OK.

Official Microsoft ISOs work unmodified. With Windows To Go, an image with more than one edition shows a list of editions; the app selects a Pro edition first and you can choose another. Keep the app in front until the burn finishes.

Which Windows versions can I write?

Windows 7, 8, 8.1, 10 and 11. The Windows Setup Customization options are for Windows 10 and 11 images.

How do I install Windows 11 on a PC without TPM 2.0 or Secure Boot?

Keep Remove requirement for 4GB+ RAM, Secure Boot and TPM 2.0. ticked in the Windows Setup Customization card. It is shown for Windows 11 images and is on by default for a standard installation. The card is not shown when the image has its own unattend file.

The same card can remove the online Microsoft account requirement, create a local account, skip the privacy questions, copy the regional options from your phone and turn off automatic BitLocker encryption. Nothing you enter leaves the phone.

My Windows ISO has a file over 4 GB. Can I still use FAT32?

Yes. That file is install.wim. Tick Split image for FAT32 compatibility. in the Advanced File System card. The app splits the file into parts so the drive can stay FAT32, and Windows setup puts the parts back together. Splitting takes a long time.

The option is shown when the image can be split, the drive is 2 TB or smaller and Windows To Go is not chosen. Without it, the app uses NTFS, which has no size limit but is not accepted by every UEFI firmware.

Where is the Windows To Go option?

Pick a Windows ISO that supports Windows To Go. The app then shows the choice between Standard Windows Installation and Windows To Go. The choice is not shown for other images or for a bare .wim file.

Windows To Go puts a portable Windows 10 or 11 on the USB drive, which then boots and runs from the drive. It uses NTFS and can take several hours: use a fast phone and a fast USB drive, turn on High-speed mode, and start with a fully charged phone.

Linux, DMG and Raspberry Pi

Which Linux images work, and when should I use GPT/UEFI or MBR?

Modern GNU/Linux distributions released after 2010 work, for example Ubuntu, Fedora, Debian and Arch. By default the image is written as it is, which uses no tokens.

Tick Advanced Options (Filesystem, Partition & Persistence) to copy the files instead and choose the file system, the Partition Table and a persistent partition (2 tokens). Choose GPT (UEFI) for PCs that start in UEFI mode and MBR (BIOS or UEFI-CSM) for legacy BIOS or CSM mode. The app preselects the table the image supports.

Persistence keeps your files and changes between boots. It is offered only for images that support it, and the app says why when it is not.

Can I burn a macOS DMG or a Raspberry Pi image?

Yes, with Disk or ISO image and no token cost. macOS DMG (Intel-based only), Raspberry Pi OS and other ARM images are written as they are. You can pick compressed .gz, .xz and .zip images without unpacking them first.

Writing, speed and safety

How long does a write take, and why can it be slow?

The speed depends on the USB drive and on the phone's USB port. A USB 3.0 drive in a USB 2.0 adapter runs at USB 2.0 speed, and many small files write much slower than one large file. Large images take time.

High-speed mode. (Recommended) is on when the phone has enough free storage for it. Windows To Go can take several hours.

Why did the write stop at a percentage or the app close during the write?
  • The app was minimised. A burn pauses while the app is in the background and continues when it is in front again.
  • No internet or an ad blocker (free version). Running burns wait until this is fixed. Pro does not pause for these.
  • The drive was removed or stopped answering. During a burn, the app shows Device Removed with Retry. Plug the same drive back in and tap Retry: the burn continues from where it stopped. A different drive is never written. If you cancel instead, the tokens come back.
  • A short connection failure. It does not stop the job: the app tries again and goes on.

A stop near the very end often means the drive reports more space than it has, or its flash fails at the end. Reconnect the drive: if the drive check fails, the app shows 🛑 CRITICAL WARNING: BAD DRIVE! and may offer to scan for the real size.

If the app was closed during a burn, the tokens come back the next time you open it.

Can I use other apps while a write is running?

No. A burn pauses when the app leaves the foreground, to protect the drive. Keep the app open and in front until it finishes. The app keeps the screen on while it is shown. Start long burns when you do not need the phone.

My PC does not boot from the USB drive. What should I check?
  1. Boot menu: select the USB drive in the PC's boot menu (often F12, F11, F9 or Esc at power-on).
  2. Secure Boot: turn it off in the BIOS/UEFI, or use an image that supports it.
  3. Partition scheme: GPT drives need UEFI mode; MBR drives need legacy or CSM mode.
  4. USB port: try a port directly on the motherboard, not on the front panel or a hub.
  5. The ISO: download it again from the official source. A truncated ISO writes without error but does not boot. DROFUS warns when an ISO looks truncated.
My drive shows less space after writing. How do I get the full capacity back?

A bootable write can leave hidden partitions. Write the drive again with Boot Selection set to Non-bootable: it formats the drive with FAT32, exFAT or NTFS (1 token).

Drive makers count in decimal gigabytes and phones in binary ones, so a "64 GB" drive holds about 59 GiB. A large shortfall can mean a counterfeit drive.

Can the app damage my USB drive?

No. Nothing the app does is unrecoverable at the hardware level; the worst case is a drive that needs formatting again. If a drive stopped working after an operation, it was already failing.

Before a burn, the app warns when an ISO looks truncated or corrupted, warns when the file is not bootable, checks the space on the drive and in phone storage, and asks for confirmation before it erases the drive.

A drive that is write-protected, or one that does not take writes, is refused at the start with one message, before any question and before any token is used.

Is the app safe, and is my data uploaded anywhere?

Your files are processed on your phone, and their contents are never uploaded. There is no account and no sign-up. The Windows setup options you enter do not leave the phone. What the app does send, such as ad requests, crash reports and purchase checks, is listed in its privacy policy.

The app asks for Bluetooth permission only on Android 8.1 and older.

Which languages is the app available in?

Many. The most recently added are Azerbaijani, Catalan, Estonian, Kazakh, Lithuanian, Latvian, Malayalam, Slovak, Slovenian, Swahili, Tagalog (Filipino) and Uzbek.

On Android 13 and later, you can choose the app's language in the phone's settings.

About DROFUS

How is DROFUS related to Rufus?

DROFUS is an independent app, not affiliated with Rufus. It is an ISO burner for Android that writes the drive from your phone, with no PC.

What happened to ISO 2 USB?

It has been retired. Every feature it had is in DROFUS, which is also known as ISO 2 USB or ISO to USB.
