Internet and ads

Why does the app need an internet connection?

The free version shows ads, and ads need the internet. While the free version is offline it shows No Internet Connection and pauses. Ads, analytics and crash reports use the internet, and all connections are encrypted.

Pro is a one-time purchase that removes all ads and lets the app work offline. Pro adds no other features.

Why does the app say an ad blocker or private DNS is active when moving files?

While an operation runs, the free version pauses if ads are blocked by an ad-blocker app, Private DNS or your network. Turn the blocker off, or set Private DNS to dns.google, then restart the app. Pro works with ad blockers and Private DNS.

Drives and file systems

Can I copy files to and from an NTFS hard drive or SSD?

Yes. NTFS drives are fully readable and writable. A drive opens read-only only when Windows hibernated it (Fast Startup), when Windows did not close it cleanly and it could not be repaired, or when the drive is write-protected or refuses writes. Shut Windows down fully (not Restart or Sleep), or check the drive in Windows, then connect it again.

Hard disks may need a powered USB hub, because a phone often cannot supply enough power on its own.

Can I read and write an ext4 drive?

Yes. The app reads and writes ext2, ext3 and ext4, as well as NTFS, exFAT, FAT12, FAT16 and FAT32.

Can I copy an ISO file to a USB drive?

Yes, like any other file. In a folder on the drive, tap Add → Import File / Files. The app checks the space before a copy starts.

A FAT32 drive cannot store a single file over 4 GB. Use a drive formatted as exFAT or NTFS for a larger ISO.

To start a PC from ISO files, use our Ventoy (Unofficial) app: it installs Ventoy on the drive, and then you copy ISO files to it.

Does it work with external SSDs on a tablet?

Yes. You can connect a USB flash drive, hard drive, SSD or card reader to a phone or tablet through an OTG cable or adapter. The phone or tablet needs Android 7.0 or newer with USB host (OTG) support. Large hard drives may need a powered USB hub.

On tablets and foldables you can use two panes side by side with drag and drop. Several drives and partitions can be open at once, also through a USB hub.

Do I need root?

Not for USB drives. Root is needed only for the phone's internal SD card slot, and your root manager must allow the app.

Copying and safety

How do I unplug the drive safely?

Wait for any running operation to finish, then tap Eject on the drive's card. When it says Safe to unplug, pull the drive out. Unplugging during a copy can damage the files being written. If the drive is unplugged during a copy, the finished files stay and no partial file is left behind.

Is it safe for my drive?

Yes. Drives that were not closed properly are checked and repaired when you connect them, and write-protected drives open read-only. The app checks the free space before a copy starts. If the drive is unplugged during a copy, the finished files stay and no partial file is left behind.

The drive's card warns you about read errors and about drives that may hold less than their stated size. An optional setting keeps the readable part of a damaged file when you copy it.

Can I pause a copy, and what happens when a file with the same name exists?

Yes, you can pause, resume or stop any operation. Copies run one after another in a queue, with live progress, speed and time left. When a file with the same name exists, choose replace, skip or keep both, for one file or for all.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Files on your drive and phone are never uploaded. They leave the device only when you share them or open them in another app. The app has no storage or location permission: it reaches phone files only through the system file picker you open.
