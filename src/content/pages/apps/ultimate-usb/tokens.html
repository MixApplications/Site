How do I earn a token?

Watch one short rewarded video ad. Each ad gives you one free token. You can also buy a pack of tokens.

If you do not have enough tokens, the app shows Not Enough Tokens with the cost and your balance, and nothing starts. Tap Get Tokens to get more.

Ads need an internet connection. Tokens are kept on your device.

When are tokens taken?

Only when you tap OK. Before a paid action starts, the app shows a warning with Tokens required: and the number. Tap OK to start, or Cancel to keep your tokens.

What happens to my tokens when an action does not complete?

They come back to you automatically:

  • when the action fails or ends with an error;
  • when you cancel a burn in the ISO Burner or a format in Format USB;
  • when you answer No to the question about overwriting the whole drive;
  • when the drive is removed or stops answering (the action then ends as failed);
  • when the app is closed during the action: the tokens are returned the next time the app starts.

Partition Wizard: if some steps finish and a later one fails, you keep paying only for the Create and Format steps that finished. The rest come back.

USB Encryption: the token comes back whenever the encryption does not succeed.

Ventoy: if a chosen Ventoy version cannot be used, nothing is written to the drive and the tokens come back. Install, Update and Clear have no Cancel button once they start.

PS2 USB Utils: if a Fix is cancelled, its token is returned the next time the app starts.

What changes with Pro?

Pro is a one-time purchase. With Pro:

  • no action uses tokens;
  • there are no ads;
  • the app works offline and with ad blockers;
  • Archive Extractor can extract straight to a USB drive or external storage.

Pro is tied to your account. Without Pro, the app needs the internet for ads, and ad blockers and blocking Private DNS must be off. If the connection drops or an ad blocker is found while a Ventoy, ISO Burner or Raw Write job runs, the job pauses.
