How do I start?

On first start, choose where the vault lives, choose a password and tap Create vault. Then write down the 12-word recovery phrase.

The vault is one encrypted file that looks like random data. Put it anywhere you can reach, such as a folder, Downloads or an SD card. Copying that file is your backup, and it opens on any phone with your password.

Can someone read my vault if they copy the vault file?

No. Strong encryption locks every file, so a copied vault file cannot be read without your password.

Where are my files stored, and how much can a vault hold?

Your files are encrypted inside the vault file, at the place you chose, or in Cofferlock's own storage if you picked Keep it inside the app. A vault inside the app is deleted with the app's data and cannot be copied with a file manager.

A free vault holds up to 5 GB. With Pro, the space is limited only by your phone storage. Any file type can go in.

How do I add files?

Use Import files, Import photos or Import a folder. A setting decides what happens to the originals: Ask (the default), Secure delete or Keep.

What happens if the app closes during an import?

Your vault is not damaged. If an import stops halfway or the app is closed, the vault stays safe, and a built-in repair tool can fix a damaged vault.

How do I get a file out?

Copy out saves the file outside the vault. The copy outside the vault is a normal, unencrypted file. You can also select a folder or several files and copy them out.

Open shows the file in another app without saving an unencrypted copy.

I forgot my password. What can I do?

On the unlock screen, tap Forgot password? Use recovery phrase, type your 12 words and choose a new password.

Without the phrase, nobody can open the vault, including us. There is no backdoor.

How do I back up my vault or move it to a new phone?

Copy the vault file with any file manager. A vault kept inside the app is copied with Settings → Copy file (free). With Pro, Back up copies it to a folder you choose and reminds you while the phone charges if the last backup is over a week old. Android cloud backup is off for vault data.

On the new phone, install Cofferlock, choose I already have a vault file and enter your password. Vault file names start with a dot, so choose Show hidden files in the file picker's menu.

Can I unlock with my fingerprint?

Yes, per vault, on Android 9 and newer. You can always unlock with your password too. If you add or remove fingerprints in Android, unlock with the password and turn Fingerprint unlock on again in Settings.

Can other people see my vault in screenshots or recent apps?

No. Screenshots are blocked, the app is hidden in recent apps, and copied text is cleared. The vault locks by itself 30 seconds after you leave the app, or when you tap Lock.

Does Cofferlock need the internet?

No for your vaults: creating, opening and using vaults never needs the internet. The app uses it only to check for a newer version, to confirm a Pro purchase with Google Play and to connect to public network time services. Your files and personal details are never sent.

Does Cofferlock show ads or track me?

No. Cofferlock has no ads, no tracking, no account and no cloud.

What happens after wrong passwords?

You must wait longer and longer: after 5 wrong tries 1 minute, after 10 tries 1 hour. This can be switched off. The Security tab shows every unlock and every failed try.

With Pro you can also choose to erase the vault after 10 or 20 wrong passwords in a row.

Can I have more than one vault?

Yes, with Pro. Several vaults and a decoy vault are part of Pro. Opening any vault file is free.

What does Pro add, and is there a subscription?

No subscription: Pro is a one-time purchase. It adds unlimited space and more than one vault, a decoy vault, an emergency erase password, a disguised app name and icon, sealed envelopes, an encrypted Trash, faster ways to add files, one-tap backup with reminders and the full security history, and more.

The Supporter edition has the same features. Restore purchase turns Pro back on for the Google account that bought it.
