What do I need?

  • An Android phone with Android 7.0 or newer that is rooted, for example with Magisk or KernelSU.
  • A USB cable that connects the phone to the computer.

Creating and preparing drives works without root. Connecting a drive to a computer needs root.

How do I use it, step by step?

  1. Tap Download APK, open the downloaded file and install Virtual Drive. Android may ask you first to allow installing apps from this source. Open the app and grant root when it asks (Grant root).
  2. Tap New drive, give the drive a name and a size, and tap Create. Your first drive becomes the active drive.
  3. Open Ultimate USB, Ventoy (Unofficial), DROFUS or USB Tools. It shows the virtual drive like a connected USB flash drive. Prepare the drive there, for example burn a Windows or Linux ISO.
  4. Go back to Virtual Drive, connect the phone to the computer with the USB cable and tap Connect to PC.
  5. Use the drive on the computer. When you are done, tap Disconnect from PC in the app, or Disconnect in its notification.

If you open one of the USB apps first and no drive exists yet, it sends you to Virtual Drive to create or choose a drive. A button there then takes you back to that app.

What does the computer see?

A normal USB drive that it can read and write. A new drive is empty: the computer shows it as an empty drive in its disk manager until you prepare it in one of the apps above or format it on the computer.

While a drive is connected to a computer, the apps cannot change it, and while an app works on the drive, it cannot be connected to a computer.

Can I have more than one drive?

Yes. Create as many drives as you need, each with its own size, and choose which one is active. The USB apps and the computer always get the active drive.

Do I have to choose any settings?

No. The app chooses by itself how to present the drive, depending on your phone. The Device check screen shows what your phone supports.

What happens when I disconnect?

The phone's USB goes back to exactly what it was before, such as file transfer or debugging. Nothing the app changes survives a restart of the phone.

Which languages does the app have?

English and 57 more, the same languages as Ultimate USB. The app follows your phone's language.
