How do I see my drive's details step by step?

  1. Connect the drive to your phone with a USB OTG adapter and open Ultimate USB or USB Tools.
  2. Open the Diagnostics & Info group and tap Device Info.
  3. Read the details. To check the partitions, tap Validate partitions.

How do I check my drive's partition table?

Tap Validate partitions. The app checks each partition and reports problems in the partition table: overlapping partitions, alignment problems and partition type mismatches.

To change partitions, use Partition Wizard. To bring back partitions that were lost, use Recover lost partitions.

Does Device Info use tokens?

No. Device Info is free.
