How do I write a disk image step by step?

  1. Connect the drive to your phone with a USB OTG adapter and open Ultimate USB.
  2. Open the Bootable & Image Writing group and tap Raw Write.
  3. Next to RAW File:, tap Pick File and choose the image (for example an .img file) in the system file picker.
  4. Tap START. The app shows All Data Will Be Permanently Deleted. Tap OK to start.
  5. The app shows Writing and the progress. Keep the app in front until it finishes.

Raw Write has no options: the drive gets an exact copy of the file. To make an installer from a Windows or Linux ISO with partition, file system and setup options, use ISO Burner.

What does the app check before it writes?

  • If the file is larger than the drive, the app shows Not Enough Size… and does not write.
  • When you connect a drive, the app checks it. If it may be fake or faulty, the app shows 🛑 CRITICAL WARNING: BAD DRIVE! and offers to scan for the size the drive really has.
  • Nothing is written before you confirm All Data Will Be Permanently Deleted.

Is Raw Write safe for my drive?

Yes. The app waits until the drive confirms the data is written before it reports success, and it protects the drive at each step:

  • A drive that is write-protected or does not take writes is recognised as soon as you start a job: one clear message, and nothing is charged.
  • If the drive did not keep its test data, it may be fake. The app says so, keeps its files read-only and offers Try restore again. Raw Write still runs after an extra Yes.
  • A job never writes to a different drive than the one it started on.
  • If the USB connection fails for a moment during a job, the app recovers it automatically and the job continues.

How many tokens does Raw Write use?

None. Raw Write is free and uses no tokens.

What happens if the write is interrupted?

  • When the app goes to the background, the write pauses, and it continues when you come back to the app. The app keeps the screen on while it is shown.
  • In the free version, the write also pauses while the phone has no internet connection or an ad blocker or blocking Private DNS is active, and continues when that is fixed. With Pro, only the background pause applies.
  • If a write error happens, the app offers Retry to go on with the write.
  • To stop the write, cancel it and answer Are you sure you want to cancel the process?
  • If the drive is unplugged or stops answering, the app shows Device Removed with Retry. Plug the same drive back in and tap Retry: the write continues where it stopped. A different drive is never written.
