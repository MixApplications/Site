Steps

  1. Open Ultimate USB, open the File & Storage group and tap Archive Extractor.
  2. Archive File: tap Pick File and choose the archive. The app shows its Size: and Type:.
  3. Out Directory: tap Pick Dir and choose where the files go.
  4. If the archive is protected, tick Use Password and type it in Archive Password.
  5. Tap START.

Which archive formats can it open?

7z, Arj, BZip2, Cab, Chm, Cpio, Deb, GZip, HFS, Iso, Lzh, Lzma, Nsis, Rar, Rpm, Split, Tar, Udf, Wim, Xar, Z and Zip. Archives split into several volumes are supported too.

Can it open password-protected archives?

Yes. Tick Use Password and enter the password in Archive Password before you tap START. The app uses it both to open the archive and to extract the files.

Does Archive Extractor use tokens?

No. Archive Extractor uses no tokens.
