Steps

  1. Connect the drive to your phone with a USB OTG adapter and open Ultimate USB or USB Tools.
  2. Open the Diagnostics & Info group and tap Benchmark.
  3. Mode: choose Sequential, Random or Both.
  4. Type: choose Read Only or Read / Write.
  5. Tap START.

Mode:Sequential, Random, BothSequential

Why is my drive slower than the speed on the box?

The whole chain limits the speed. A USB 3.0 drive in a USB 2.0 adapter runs at USB 2.0 speed. The figures on the box are usually peak read speed, not sustained write speed.

Does Benchmark use tokens?

No. Benchmark is free.

Benchmark does not change your files, in either mode. Results are shown in MB/s.

If the drive gives back different data when the same place is read again, the test stops.

To check whether the flash itself is failing, run Bad Blocks Check. It erases the drive.
