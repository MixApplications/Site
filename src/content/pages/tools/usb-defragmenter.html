How do I defragment a USB drive step by step?

  1. Connect the drive to your phone with a USB OTG adapter and open Ultimate USB or USB Tools.
  2. Open the Disk Management group and tap USB Defragmenter.
  3. Partition: pick the partition. Only FAT32 and exFAT partitions are listed.
  4. Tap START. The app goes through Scanning the drive…, Analyzing files…, Moving files… and Finishing…, and shows SUCCESS at the end.

If the drive has no FAT32 or exFAT partition, the app shows No Partitions Found!. If the drive is nearly full, it shows Low Free Space; tap Run Anyway to go on, or Cancel.

Why defragment a USB drive?

It puts the pieces of fragmented files back together. Open PS2 Loader (OPL) needs unfragmented game files. For PS2 games you can also defragment single games in PS2 USB Utils.

When does the defragmenter refuse to run?

It does not start, or it stops, and tells you why when:

  • the file table of the partition cannot be read,
  • the phone does not have enough memory,
  • the drive changed during the run,
  • the drive was not closed properly and has to be checked first,
  • the drive is smaller than it reports,
  • the drive is too full.

Where the drive can be repaired, the app offers its own check.

How many tokens does defragmenting use?

None. USB Defragmenter is free.

What if I stop it or unplug the drive?

  • To stop, tap cancel and confirm Are you sure you want to cancel the process?
  • If you leave the app, the data written so far is saved to the drive, and the run waits until you are back in the app.
  • If the drive is unplugged, the app shows The drive was disconnected. Files moved so far are safe. Plug it in and run Defragment again.
