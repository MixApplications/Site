How do I wipe a USB drive step by step?

  1. Connect the drive to your phone with a USB OTG adapter and open Ultimate USB or USB Tools.
  2. Open the Disk Management group and tap Wipe USB. All data and all partitions on the drive will be deleted.
  3. Method: choose how the drive is overwritten (see the table below).
  4. Verification: choose None, Sampled (recommended) or Full.
  5. Check the Estimated time. The app says what it is based on: this drive's speed measured in this session, a short read test of this drive, or a cautious default speed.
  6. Tap START. If the wipe will take more than one hour, the app shows Long erasure: the app has to stay open in the foreground all that time. Tap Start to go on.
  7. The app shows the drive's vendor, product and serial number and says that everything on the drive will be overwritten and that this cannot be undone. Check that it is the right drive, then confirm.
  8. While it runs, the app shows Wiping · pass x of y and Do not disconnect the drive. With verification, it then shows Verifying and checks the drive.
  9. At the end the result card shows Erasure complete, Erasure incomplete or Erasure failed. Tap View erasure certificate or View erasure report and save it.

A drive that is write-protected or does not take writes is refused before the app asks you anything.

Which wipe methods can I choose?

Five methods. The default is NIST SP 800-88 Clear (1 pass), and the app remembers your choice for the next wipe.

MethodWhat each pass writesVerification
NIST SP 800-88 Clear (1 pass)Zeros (0x00)As chosen
Australian ISM, flash media (2 passes)Random data, then random data againAlways full
DoD 5220.22-M (3 passes)0x00, then 0xFF, then random dataAlways full
Bruce Schneier (7 passes)0xFF, then 0x00, then random data five timesAs chosen
Peter Gutmann (35 passes)4 random passes, 27 fixed patterns in random order, then 4 random passesAs chosen

How does the app verify the wipe?

It checks the drive after writing. The default is Sampled (recommended). Australian ISM and DoD 5220.22-M always use Full, whatever you select.

VerificationWhat it does
NoneNo check. The report states that no check was performed.
Sampled (recommended)Checks samples from across the whole drive. Adds about ten seconds. On a very small drive, the whole drive is checked.
FullChecks the whole drive. The app shows how much time this adds for your drive.

The wipe covers the writable size of the drive. On a drive that reports more space than it really stores, only the writable part is overwritten, and the report says so.

What is in the erasure report and certificate?

The Data erasure report describes the wipe in detail. The Certificate of erasure is issued only for a completed erasure. Both are made on your phone, offline, and go nowhere unless you share them. They contain:

  • Header: Report ID, issue date and the time it was generated. Each page footer shows the page number and the report ID.
  • Device: the drive's maker, model and serial number, its Reported capacity and its Writable capacity. A drive that stores less than it claims shows a smaller writable capacity.
  • Sanitization method: the erasure method and standard, and each pass.
  • Result: whether it completed, stopped or failed; start and finish time, duration, data written, errors, and every pause and continue.
  • Verification: whether the check passed, failed or was not done.
  • Scope and limitations: what a wipe over USB can and cannot reach (see below).
  • Your details (optional): name, title, organization, location, contact, asset number, media source, an attestation, and a place to sign and date. Tap Apply to add them. They are kept on your phone only and remembered for the next wipe.
  • A disclaimer.

How do I save the report?

Tap Save to store it as a PDF in a place you pick, Share to send it, or Export as plain text. Turn on Open this automatically after a wipe to see it every time a wipe ends.

Save the report before you leave. It cannot be produced again later. If you leave without saving, the app shows Report not saved with Save now and Discard.

What can a wipe over USB not reach?

The report states these limits openly:

  • The wipe is at the NIST "Clear" level, not "Purge" or "Destroy".
  • Hidden spare areas inside a flash drive cannot be reached from a phone.
  • Device identifiers are shown as the device reports them.

Is wiping safe for my drive?

Yes. The app can read the drive back to check the wipe, and it erases only the drive you confirmed:

  • If the drive did not keep its test data, it may be fake. The app says so, keeps its files read-only and offers Try restore again. A wipe still runs after an extra Yes.
  • A job never writes to a different drive than the one it started on.

How many tokens does a wipe use?

None. Wipe USB is free with every method.

Can I pause a wipe or continue it later?

  • Yes. Tap Pause. The app saves its position, and nothing is written to the drive until you tap Continue. It then goes on from the same pass and the same place.
  • If you leave the app, the wipe is paused the same way and continues when the app is in front again. Without Pro, it is also paused while the phone is offline or an ad blocker is active.
  • If the wipe is stopped, the drive is removed or the app is closed, the wipe stays saved. When you connect the same drive again, the app shows Continue wipe with Continue and Discard.
  • If you discard a saved wipe, the drive stays partly erased, and a new wipe starts from the beginning.
  • If the connection to the drive fails for a moment, the app recovers it and goes on. The report lists these as bus recoveries.
