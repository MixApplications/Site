How do I check a drive for bad blocks step by step?

  1. Copy any files you want to keep off the drive. The check destroys all data on it.
  2. Connect the drive to your phone with a USB OTG adapter and open Ultimate USB or USB Tools.
  3. Open the Diagnostics & Info group and tap Bad Blocks Check.
  4. Number of Passes: choose 1 Pass or 2 Passes.
  5. Keep Detect fake / counterfeit capacity ticked to test the real size of the drive.
  6. Tap START. The app warns that all data will be destroyed. Confirm to start.

Keep the app in the foreground while the check runs: operations pause when the app is minimized, to protect the drive.

A drive that is write-protected or does not take writes is refused before the app asks you anything.

What does Bad Blocks Check find?

  • Failing sectors: places on the drive that do not keep data correctly are reported as bad blocks.
  • Fake-capacity drives: a counterfeit USB drive or SD card reports a large capacity but silently discards anything past its real size. The check exposes this.

When you connect a drive that may be fake, the app also offers to scan for the size the drive really has.

What do I do if the check finds errors?

Replace the drive. Physical flash failure cannot be repaired by any software.

Does Bad Blocks Check use tokens?

No. Bad Blocks Check is free. The app only asks you to confirm before it starts.

To measure the drive's speed without erasing it, use Benchmark with Read Only.
