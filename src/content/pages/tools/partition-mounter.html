How do I mount a partition step by step?

  1. Connect the drive to your phone with a USB OTG adapter and open Ultimate USB.
  2. Open the File & Storage group and tap Partition Mounter.
  3. Partition: choose the partition.
  4. Mode: choose Read Only or Read / Write.
  5. Tap Mount.
  6. Tap Open Files App. The system Files app opens, and the partition is there.
  7. When you are done, tap Unmount, then unplug the drive.

If the drive refuses writes, the app mounts it read-only, even when you chose Read / Write.

Can other apps open the files on the drive?

Yes. While the partition is mounted, other apps can open its files through Android's file picker. With Read / Write, they can also save to it.

Why can't I go back or eject the drive?

While a partition is mounted, the app refuses Back and Eject and shows Unmount partition first. Tap Unmount, then go back or eject. While mounted, the partition, the mode and Mount cannot be changed; only Unmount is available.

Does Partition Mounter use tokens?

No. Partition Mounter is free.

To browse and manage files inside Ultimate USB instead, use USB File Manager.
