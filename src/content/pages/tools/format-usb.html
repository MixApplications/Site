How do I format a USB drive step by step?

  1. Connect the drive to your phone with a USB OTG adapter and open Ultimate USB or USB Tools.
  2. Open the Disk Management group and tap Format USB.
  3. File System: choose FAT16, FAT32, exFAT, NTFS, EXT2, EXT3 or EXT4.
  4. Cluster Size: keep the size the app selects for your drive, or pick another one from the list. Each file system has its own default, and the format uses the size you pick.
  5. Partition Table: choose MBR or GPT.
  6. Volume Label: type a name for the drive (up to 32 characters).
  7. Tap Format. The app shows All Data Will Be Permanently Deleted and how many tokens the format uses. Tap OK to start.

The whole drive becomes one partition with the file system you chose.

If the drive is read-only, the app tells you so.

What does the app check after formatting?

Before it reports success, the app tests the drive it has just formatted.

If the test fails, the format is reported as failed, not as a success.

Is formatting safe for my drive?

Yes. The app tests every format before it reports success, and it protects the drive at each step:

  • A drive that is write-protected or does not take writes is recognised as soon as you start a job: one clear message, and nothing is charged.
  • If the drive did not keep its test data, it may be fake. The app says so, keeps its files read-only and offers Try restore again. A format still runs after an extra Yes; if you answer No, the token comes back.
  • If you kept the claimed size of a drive that may be fake, the app warns you once before it writes, with Continue and Cancel. If you cancel, nothing is written and no token is used.
  • A job never writes to a different drive than the one it started on.
  • If the USB connection fails for a moment during a job, the app recovers it automatically and the job continues.
  • While a format runs, the Back button does not stop it. Use Cancel to stop a format.

How many tokens does formatting use?

A format uses 1 token. The app shows the cost before it starts, and you earn a token by watching one short rewarded video. With Pro, formatting uses no tokens.

The token comes back automatically when the drive is removed before the start, when you answer No, when the format fails or is cancelled, and when the app is closed during the format (it is returned the next time the app starts).

Can I stop a format that is running?

Yes, with every file system. Tap Cancel and confirm. The app then makes sure the unfinished volume is not opened by mistake, and the token comes back. Format the drive again to use it. If the drive is unplugged during a format, the app reports Device Removed and the token comes back too.

Keep the app open until the format finishes.
