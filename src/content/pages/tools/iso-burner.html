Steps

  1. Connect the drive to your phone with a USB OTG adapter. Open DROFUS, which starts on the ISO Burner screen, or in Ultimate USB open the Bootable & Image Writing group and tap ISO Burner.
  2. Boot Selection: keep Disk or ISO image to write an image file, or choose Non-bootable, MS-DOS or FreeDOS. Changing it resets your other choices.
  3. Tap Pick ISO and choose the image. The app shows Reading ISO File and its progress while it reads the image; you can stop this with Cancel.
  4. Check ISO Label: and Size:, then the options the app shows for this image. It sets their defaults from the image.
  5. Tap START. The app shows All Data Will Be Permanently Deleted and how many tokens the burn uses. Tap OK to start.
  6. Keep the app in front until the burn finishes.

DROFUS is an independent app, not affiliated with Rufus.

How do I make a Windows 10 or Windows 11 installer?

Pick the Windows ISO and keep Standard Windows Installation. Check the partition table and file system the app sets from the image, choose the Windows Setup Customization options you want, and tap START. Windows 7, 8, 8.1, 10 and 11 images are supported, and official Microsoft ISOs work unmodified.

  • Partition Table: GPT (UEFI) or MBR (BIOS or UEFI-CSM). The app preselects one from the image and locks the choice when the image allows only one.
  • File System: FAT32 or NTFS. When the image has a file over 4 GB, tick Split image for FAT32 compatibility. in the Advanced File System card so the image fits on FAT32. Without the tick the app uses NTFS, which not every UEFI firmware accepts.
  • The split option is shown when the image can be split, the drive is 2 TB or smaller and you are not making Windows To Go. It turns off when you change the file system away from FAT32.
  • High-speed mode. (Recommended) uses free space in phone storage. If there is not enough, the app shows how much is required and how much is available, and START stays disabled until you turn the mode off or free up space.

What is Windows To Go, and where is the option?

Windows To Go puts a portable Windows 10 or 11 on the USB drive that runs from the drive itself. After you pick the Windows ISO, choose Windows To Go instead of Standard Windows Installation. The choice appears only for images that support Windows To Go, and not for a bare .wim file.

  • If the image has more than one edition, choose the edition in the list that appears. The app preselects the first Pro or Professional edition that is not Education or Workstation.
  • The file system is NTFS, and Set the target's internal drives offline is on by default.
  • The app warns that Windows To Go is a very long process that may take several hours. Use a high-end phone, a high-speed USB drive and High-speed mode. (Recommended), and charge the phone fully before you start.

How do I write a Linux ISO, and can I add persistence?

By default the app writes a Linux ISO as it is, keeping its original layout. This uses no tokens.

To choose the file system and partition table yourself, or to add persistence, tick Advanced Options (Filesystem, Partition & Persistence). The app then copies the image's files to the drive instead, which uses 2 tokens. The tick is shown only for ISOs that can be written both ways.

  • Persistent partition size: move the slider from Disabled to add a persistent partition. It keeps your files and changes between boots.
  • Persistence file system: ext3 or ext4. It is shown when a size is set and more than one file system is possible.
  • Where persistence is not possible, the app shows a note instead of the slider: the image does not support it, the distribution sets it up itself, or the image is written as it is. Persistence also needs enough free space on the drive.

Can I write a macOS DMG or a Raspberry Pi image?

Yes. Keep Disk or ISO image and pick the file. The app writes macOS DMG images (Intel-based), Raspberry Pi OS and other ARM images as they are, with no file system or partition options. These writes use no tokens. An image inside an archive can be written too.

How do I make an MS-DOS or FreeDOS drive, or just format the drive?

Choose MS-DOS or FreeDOS under Boot Selection and tap START. No image file is needed. This uses 1 token.

Choose Non-bootable to format the drive without making it bootable. Set Partition Table:, File System: and Cluster Size:, then tap START. This uses 1 token. It also gives back the full space of a drive after a bootable write.

What does the app check before it writes?

The app checks the drive and the image first, and it stops or asks you when something is wrong:

  • Fake capacity: the app checks the drive when you connect it. If it may be fake or faulty, the app shows 🛑 CRITICAL WARNING: BAD DRIVE! and offers to scan for the size the drive really has. A burn on such a drive is refused until the real size is set; otherwise use a different drive.
  • Drive cannot be used: if the app cannot read or write the drive, it shows Failed to read USB device. and asks you to reinsert the drive. The drive may be write-protected, or the cable or connector may be faulty.
  • Write-protected drive: a drive that is write-protected or does not take writes is refused at the start, with one message, before any question and before any token is used.
  • Drive too small: the app shows Not enough space on the drive. with the space needed and the space available. It counts the drive size minus 100 MB as available.
  • Drives over 2 TB: MBR and FAT32 cannot be used, and the app says so.
  • Truncated or corrupt ISO: when an ISO looks truncated or corrupted, which usually means the download was interrupted, the app says so and asks whether to continue anyway. An image that is incomplete or damaged is refused, with a message to download it again.
  • Checking image...: before the drive is erased, the app checks the image. An image that is incomplete or cannot be read is refused, and nothing is written to the drive.
  • Not bootable: the app shows The selected file is not a bootable image. and asks whether to continue anyway. A file it cannot use shows Unsupported File.
  • No BIOS boot loader: if the image has no boot loader for BIOS, the drive is finished as UEFI-only, and the app shows a warning.
  • File no longer there: if the image cannot be opened when you tap START, the app shows Cannot Open File.
  • File cannot be picked: the app shows Storage is not ready, or Pick the file from Internal storage, or copy it first.
  • Phone storage: with High-speed mode on, the app checks the free space in phone storage first.
  • Erase confirmation: nothing is written before you confirm All Data Will Be Permanently Deleted.

Why did the burn fail, and what do I do?

When a burn cannot finish, the message names the cause:

  • The drive is write-protected.
  • The image is incomplete or damaged. Download it again.
  • The WIM file cannot be split for FAT32. Choose NTFS.
  • The drive did not keep the copy. It may be counterfeit.
  • NTFS could not be started. Choose FAT32 or exFAT.
  • The phone does not have enough memory.

The app shows the cost before it starts, and you earn a token by watching one short rewarded video. With Pro, burning uses no tokens. If you do not have enough tokens, the app shows Not Enough Tokens with a Get Tokens button, and nothing starts.

The tokens come back automatically when the burn fails, is cancelled or stops with an error, and when the app is closed during the burn (they are returned the next time the app starts).

How long does a burn take?

It depends on the speed of the USB drive and of the phone's USB port. Turn on High-speed mode. (Recommended) when the app offers it. Windows To Go can take several hours.

What happens if the burn is interrupted?

Keep the app in front. When it goes to the background, Windows, Linux and raw-image burns pause, and they continue when you come back to the app while it is still running. The app keeps the screen on while it is shown.

In the free version, a running burn also pauses while the phone has no internet connection or an ad blocker or blocking Private DNS is active, and continues when that is fixed. With Pro, only the background pause applies.

If the drive is removed or stops answering, the burn ends and the app shows The drive was removed or stopped answering. Reconnect it and try again. The tokens come back. Reconnect the drive and start the burn again.

If you plug in a different drive during a burn, the app never writes to it.
