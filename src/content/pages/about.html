How our apps work

  • Private: your files are processed on your phone and never uploaded, and no account is needed.
  • Every app is free to use. Most are supported by ads; Cofferlock has none. Where an app has a paid upgrade, it is a one-time purchase — never a subscription.

How we test our USB apps

Every version is fully tested before each release, on a wide range of phones from different brands (Android 10 to 16) and a large, varied set of USB storage devices, including flash drives, SD card readers and external hard disks, used directly and through USB hubs.

Each drive is tested when you connect it. Read-only and fake drives are caught, and nothing is erased without your OK.
