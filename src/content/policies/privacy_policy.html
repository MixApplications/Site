This Privacy Policy applies to the apps named above, developed and published by MixApplications ("we," "us," or "our"). By downloading or using any of them, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. General Overview & Local-First Processing

Our apps perform their utility tasks locally on your device. The content of the files, disk images, drives and themes you work on (formatting, burning, copying, conversion, theme building and similar operations) is handled on your device. We do not upload, collect or receive the contents of your personal files or disk images.

The following limited exceptions apply, each described in the sections referenced:

  • Crash reports. If an error or crash occurs, the crash-reporting service may record diagnostic information that includes the name or path of the file, drive location or document involved, or the title of a game being processed (see Section 10).
  • Purchase checks. In Ultimate USB, DROFUS, Ventoy, USB Tools and USB File Manager, a purchase identifier, the app’s package name and a Google-issued purchase token (never file content) are sent to our server-side functions to confirm and complete the purchase with Google Play (see Section 2). ChaoMe checks its purchase on the device.
  • Game artwork. In the PS2 tools of Ultimate USB and USB Tools, the identifiers of games found on your drive (such as a disc serial) are sent to our artwork server to download the game list and cover art. File contents are never sent.
  • Downloads you start. In Ultimate USB and Ventoy, the built-in browser and ISO download fetch files directly from the website you choose; those requests go to that website, not to us. The Ventoy theme gallery opens the theme website in your browser.
  • Update notices. Ultimate USB, DROFUS, Ventoy, USB Tools and USB File Manager ask Google Firebase Remote Config whether a newer version is available or required.
  • App integrity. Our apps use Google Play Integrity through Firebase App Check to confirm that requests come from a genuine copy of the app.

2. In-App Purchases and Tokens

All financial transactions and in-app purchases are processed by the Google Play Billing system. We do not directly collect or store your credit card or financial information. In apps that use tokens (coins), the token balance is stored on your device.

In Ultimate USB, DROFUS, Ventoy, USB Tools and USB File Manager, to confirm a purchase’s validity (for example, to restore your Pro entitlement after reinstalling the app), the product identifier, the app’s package name and a Google-issued purchase token are sent to our server-side functions on Google Cloud (Firebase Cloud Functions). They check the purchase against Google Play’s records and, where needed, acknowledge or consume it with Google Play. These functions do not store the data they receive in a database (see Section 3). ChaoMe Theme Editor has no server: it verifies the purchase signature on your device.

3. Data Retention and Deletion Policy

We do not maintain a database of user profiles. Purchase identifiers and tokens are processed transiently by our server-side functions solely to confirm and complete the purchase with Google Play; they are not written to a database, though, like any server request, they may briefly appear in our cloud provider’s operational logs. Requests to our artwork server are answered and not linked to any user. Data collected through third-party SDKs is retained for a limited period and then deleted automatically: Crashlytics data (crash stack traces and associated identifiers) is retained for 90 days, and Firebase Analytics data (both event data and user data) is retained for 2 months.

Right to Request Deletion: You have the right to request deletion of personal data we process. Uninstalling the app removes all data stored locally on your device, including any token balance; if Android backup is switched on, Android may keep a copy of app data in your Google account’s device backup, which you manage in your Google account settings. To request deletion of data tied to SDK-level identifiers (such as your Advertising ID), you may reset that identifier in your device’s Android settings, or contact us using the details in Section 12.

8. Age-Appropriate Experience & Children's Privacy

The declared target audience of these apps on Google Play is users aged 18 and older, and they are not designed or marketed to attract children. Because app stores cannot fully prevent younger users from installing an app, we have built in additional safeguards for the possibility that a user is a minor. Where available, we use signals from Google Play’s Age Signals API (see Section 10) to provide an age-appropriate experience:

  • Users identified as under 18 are shown only non-personalized, contextual ads, and our ad and consent requests carry Google’s and our mediation partner’s child-protection signals (such as “under the age of consent” or child-directed treatment).
  • In-app purchases are blocked for users identified as under 13. For users identified as 13 to 17, purchases either require an additional parental check or are blocked, depending on the app.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is necessary to provide these safeguards, and the age signal is kept in memory for the current session only; it is not stored by us (see Section 10).

11. Security

Data sent by our apps to Firebase, Google Play, our server-side functions and our artwork server travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Token balances and settings are kept in the app’s private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Our server-side functions keep no database of users or purchases, so there is no store of personal data on our side that could be breached. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we limit what leaves your device to what is described in this policy.
