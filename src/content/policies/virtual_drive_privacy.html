This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile app Virtual Drive (the “App”), published by MixApplications ("we," "us," or "our"), handles information. By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Overview

Virtual Drive creates virtual drives on your phone and shows the active one to a computer connected with a USB cable, as a USB flash drive. It contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting software, and it has no user accounts.

The App uses the internet only for the following.

Update check. When the App starts, it asks Google Firebase Remote Config whether a newer version of Virtual Drive is available or required. As Google documents for Firebase (Firebase data disclosure), Firebase collects with it a random Firebase installation identifier (created for this installation of the App; Google states it does not uniquely identify a user or a physical device), your device’s model, brand and Android version, its country, language and time zone, and the App’s package name and version. The answer is only version information. See also Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Time check. If your phone’s automatic date and time is turned off, the App reads the current date from public internet time servers (such as time.google.com or pool.ntp.org). The request contains no information about you.

If a version is no longer supported, the App asks you to update it; your drives are not changed. Every internet request reveals your IP address to the server that answers it; we do not store it. We do not receive your drives or their contents, file or folder names, usage data, location or contacts.

2. Where Your Data Is

  • Your virtual drives (each one a file with the name and size you chose) and everything written to them are stored in the App’s private storage on your phone, where file managers and other apps cannot see them. The App never uploads them.
  • App settings, which drive is active, and the connection state and log of the App’s USB helper are kept in the same private storage.
  • We have no copy of your drives and cannot see, open or recover them.

3. Permissions and Root Access

Root access. Connecting a drive to a computer needs root access, which you grant in your root manager (for example Magisk or KernelSU). The App uses it only on your phone: to show the active drive to the computer over USB and to put the phone’s USB settings back afterwards. Creating and preparing drives works without root.

Notifications. While a drive is connected to a computer, the App shows a notification with a Disconnect button; on Android 13 and later it asks for the notification permission for this. For the same connection it uses a foreground service and keeps the phone awake (wake lock) while the computer uses the drive.

Internet. The App declares the internet permission for the connections described in Section 1. The Google Firebase library may add its own standard entries, as its documentation describes. The App requests no storage, camera, microphone, location or contacts permission.

4. Other Apps and the Computer

Our USB apps (Ultimate USB, Ventoy (Unofficial), DROFUS, ISO 2 USB and USB Tools) can read and write the active drive through the App, on your phone, so that you can prepare it there; other apps are refused. Nothing is sent to us by this.

While a drive is connected, the computer can read and write everything on that drive, like any USB flash drive. What the computer does with it is governed by that computer’s own software and settings.

5. Device Report You Choose to Share

The Device check screen has a Send report button. The report contains the App version, the device manufacturer and model, the Android and kernel versions, the connection state, the results of the device check and the log of the App’s USB helper. It leaves your phone only if you tap Send report and send it with an app you choose (for example your email app). Nothing is ever sent automatically. We use a report you send only to fix the problem you reported.

6. Support

If you contact us through our contact page, by email or through WhatsApp, we receive what you send (such as your email address or phone number and your message) and use it only to answer you.

7. Backups

Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer of the App’s data are disabled, so your drives and settings are not copied to Google or to another phone. To keep a copy of what is on a drive, copy its files yourself while it is connected to a computer.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not maintain a database of users.

  • On-device data (your drives, settings and the helper’s log) stays on your phone. Deleting a drive in the App removes it and everything on it; uninstalling the App or clearing its data removes all of it permanently, and nobody, including us, can recover it.
  • Support conversations and device reports you send us are kept only as long as needed to resolve your request and are deleted when the support case is closed.
  • The Firebase installation identifier is kept by Google Firebase as Google describes in Privacy and Security in Firebase; uninstalling the App ends its use. We do not store it.

Deletion requests: we do not offer a data deletion service. We keep no user database and no account, and the App sends us nothing that we keep, so we hold no data from the App that we can find and delete for one person. Support conversations and device reports you send us are deleted when the support case is closed (above). Uninstalling the App or clearing its data removes everything it keeps on your phone.

15. Security

Your drives are kept in the App’s private storage, which other apps cannot read; only our USB apps can reach the active drive through the App (Section 4). The update check uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS). While a drive is connected, anyone using that computer can read and change what is on it, as with any USB flash drive, so connect your phone only to computers you trust and disconnect when you are done. No method of protection is perfect.
