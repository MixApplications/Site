Privacy Policy — MixMedia

This policy applies to the MixMedia app (video, audio, photo, GIF and subtitle converter and editor) published by MixApplications. It is specific to MixMedia; other MixApplications apps are covered by the general privacy policy.

Last updated: October 24, 2026
The short version

MixMedia compresses, converts, trims, merges and edits your videos, music, photos, GIFs and subtitles entirely on your device. Your media files are never uploaded, and MixMedia has no account and no cloud. The only information that leaves your device is what the advertising SDKs need to show ads (with your consent where the law requires it), the update check and usage statistics sent to Google Firebase (never a file, its name or its location), the ad-availability check (no personal data), the age range Google Play may share, and the Google Play purchase token that is checked with Google Play if you buy Remove ads.

1. Overview: Your Media Stays on Your Device

Everything MixMedia does with your media happens on your phone or tablet: compressing and converting video, extracting and converting audio, trimming, cutting and merging, converting and resizing photos, making GIFs and handling subtitles. The code that processes media has no network access. MixApplications never receives your files, their contents, their names or their locations.

MixMedia has no account, no sign-in and no cloud storage of its own. We do not use third-party attribution or tracking services; Firebase Analytics reads the Google Play install referrer, as Firebase documents. To keep the app working and up to date we use Google Firebase: Remote Config (tells the app whether a newer version is available and how often full-screen ads may appear) and Analytics (which screens are used; no advertising ID, ad-personalization signals turned off; in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland it stores data on your device only if you consented in the ad privacy form). Neither ever receives a media file, its contents, its name or its location.

2. Files and Permissions

  • Files you open are read through Android’s system file picker, the photo picker, “Share” or “Open with”; MixMedia sees only the files and folders you choose.
  • Files MixMedia creates are written as new files in Movies/MixMedia, Music/MixMedia, Pictures/MixMedia or Documents/MixMedia, or where you choose. Your original file is never changed unless you explicitly choose “Replace original” and confirm it.
  • App data (the job queue, recent items, settings) is stored only in the app’s private storage on your device.
  • Permissions: internet and network state (used only by the advertising SDKs, Google Firebase, the purchase check, Google Play and the ad-availability check described in Section 3; the free version needs an internet connection), notifications, a foreground service and wake lock (progress of long jobs such as converting a video), vibration (touch feedback), Google Play billing (Remove ads) and the advertising ID (ads). MixMedia does not ask for camera, microphone, location, contacts or phone permissions. The advertising and Google SDKs add further entries to the app’s permission list as their documentation describes (for example Wi-Fi state, ad-services and install-referrer access, and storage on old Android versions); MixMedia never asks you for them and does not use them itself.

3. How Ads and Consent Work

Ads are provided through the Appodeal mediation platform and the advertising networks reachable through it (listed in Section 12). Before any ad is requested, MixMedia asks Appodeal’s Consent Management Platform together with Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) whether a consent prompt is required for you (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, or under US state laws), and shows it when needed. Your choice is respected by the networks: without consent, only non-personalized ads are requested where the law requires it.

The free version of MixMedia needs an internet connection so that ads can be shown; when you are offline it asks you to connect or to buy Remove ads, which works offline. Jobs that are already running keep running. While a free user has the app open, MixMedia also checks whether ads can reach your device: it reads whether the device is online and the name of a Private DNS server if one is set, and it tries to reach a few advertising servers (it sends them no data about you or your media). If ads are blocked, MixMedia asks you to allow them or to buy Remove ads. These results stay on your device and are not sent to us. MixMedia never reads the network’s traffic and never interrupts work you already started.

Where ads appear (free version only): banner and native ads, marked as ads, on the Home, Tools and Open screens; a medium rectangle and a native ad on the Jobs screen while a job runs; a medium rectangle on a job’s result screen; and a full-screen ad when you add a job to the queue or leave a finished result, no more often than the limits set in Remote Config. Ads never appear inside a tool’s settings, a preview, Settings or dialogs. The advertising SDKs process your device’s advertising ID, technical device and network information (including IP address, from which an approximate location may be derived) and ad interactions, under their own privacy policies. They never receive your media or its contents.

MixMedia shows ads in its free version. Google AdMob and the other advertising partners named in this policy may serve ads, collect information directly from your device, and use your device’s advertising ID (and, inside the web content of an ad, cookies or similar technologies) to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure their performance, prevent fraud and, where you have consented or the law allows, personalize them. To learn how Google uses this information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can choose whether Google shows you personalized ads in My Ad Center, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads on some devices).

4. Remove Ads (Purchase)

Remove ads is a one-time purchase that turns off all ads; every tool stays available to everyone, free or not. The purchase is processed by Google Play under Google’s terms. MixMedia never sees your payment details. To confirm that a purchase is genuine, the app sends the product identifier, the Google Play purchase token and the app’s package name to our purchase-check service (Cloud Functions for Firebase, run by MixApplications). The service asks Google Play whether the purchase is valid, completes (acknowledges) it with Google Play if needed, and returns only the purchase status. It does not store the token; Google Cloud keeps its standard request logs (such as time and IP address). The app then keeps the Google Play purchase record in its private storage on your device so that ads stay off offline. When MixMedia starts, it may ask Google Play for your age range (Play Age Signals; Google Play may ask you first whether to share it). If Google Play reports that you are under 18, only non-personalized, child-directed ads are requested; under 13, purchases are turned off; from 13 to 17, a parent must confirm a purchase. Only the resulting setting is used, on the device. When Remove ads is active the advertising SDKs are not started.

5. Data Security, Retention and Deletion

Security. Media is processed on your device and is never uploaded. Temporary working files stay in the app’s private storage and are removed when the job ends (or when it is cancelled). Traffic of the Google Play and Firebase SDKs is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Advertising SDKs send ad requests over encrypted connections too. MixMedia refuses every unencrypted (plain HTTP) connection, so ad content such as images and video is also loaded only over HTTPS. MixMedia has no account and keeps no copy of your data on any server.

Everything MixMedia itself stores lives on your device. Usage statistics are kept by Google Analytics for Firebase for 2 months and then deleted. You can delete all app data by uninstalling MixMedia or using Android Settings → Apps → MixMedia → Storage → Clear storage. Files you saved are your own files and stay where you saved them.

Data collected by the advertising SDKs is retained by those providers according to their own policies (see Section 12). Because MixApplications does not receive identifiers from them, a deletion request for advertising data is best made directly to the provider; you can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android settings.

Deletion requests: we do not offer a data deletion service. MixMedia has no account and we keep no user database, so we hold no data from the app that we can find and delete for one person. Usage statistics are deleted automatically as described above; to limit advertising data, use the steps above or More → Ad privacy (Section 6).

6. Your Choices and Controls

  • Ad privacy choices: open More → Ad privacy in MixMedia at any time to review or change your consent (the row is shown where the law in your region requires a consent choice, and not after Remove ads, which shows no ads).
  • No ads at all: buy Remove ads.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Notifications: turn them off in Android settings; jobs still run.

7. European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the EU/EEA or the UK, your personal data is processed in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK GDPR. The legal bases are Consent (for personalized advertising, and for usage statistics in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), Legitimate Interests (for the update check, delivering non-personalized ads and preventing fraud and invalid traffic), Contract (to provide Remove ads after a purchase) and Legal obligation (applying the age rules for younger users). You have:

  • The right to request access to the personal data that we process.
  • The right to correct inaccurate personal data.
  • The right to request deletion of your personal data.
  • The right to restrict or object to processing, and to data portability.
  • The right to withdraw your consent at any time (Section 6).
  • The right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.
