Privacy Policy — MixDocs

This policy applies to the MixDocs app (documents, PDF and scanner) published by MixApplications. It is specific to MixDocs; other MixApplications apps are covered by the general privacy policy.

Last updated: October 2, 2026
The short version

MixDocs opens, edits, converts, scans and recognises text in your documents entirely on your device. Your files, scans and the text in them are never uploaded, and MixDocs has no account and no cloud. The only information that leaves your device is what the advertising SDKs need to show ads (with your consent where the law requires it), the update check, crash reports and usage statistics sent to Google Firebase (never a document, its name or its location), and the Google Play purchase receipt that is checked with Google Play if you buy MixDocs Pro, which removes all ads.

1. Overview: Your Documents Stay on Your Device

Everything MixDocs does with your documents happens on your phone or tablet: viewing and editing PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint and OpenDocument files, converting, merging, compressing, redacting, signing, scanning paper, and recognising text (OCR). The code that processes documents has no network access. MixApplications never receives your files, your scans, the text inside them, their names or your search terms.

MixDocs has no account, no sign-in and no cloud storage of its own. We do not use attribution services. To keep the app working and up to date we use Google Firebase: Remote Config (tells the app whether a newer version is available), Crashlytics (crash reports with document names, paths and addresses removed) and Analytics (which screens are used; no advertising ID, ad-personalization signals turned off). None of them ever receives a document, its contents, its name or its location.

2. Files, Scans and Permissions

  • Files you open are read through Android’s system file picker or “Open with”; MixDocs sees only the files and folders you choose. Folders you add can be removed at any time in the app.
  • Files MixDocs creates (converted files, copies you save, scans) are written as new files in Download/MixDocs or where you choose. Your original file is never changed unless you explicitly choose “Replace original”.
  • Scanning uses Google’s on-device document scanner (ML Kit); pages are processed on the device.
  • Library data (recent files, thumbnails, reading position, settings) is stored only in the app’s private storage on your device.
  • Permissions: internet and network state (used only by the advertising SDKs, Google Firebase, the purchase check, Google Play and a standard network time request (it asks a public time server for the current time; the server sees only your IP address); the free version needs an internet connection), notifications, a foreground service and wake lock (progress of long tasks such as converting or recognising text), vibration (touch feedback), Google Play billing (MixDocs Pro) and the advertising ID (ads). MixDocs does not request storage, camera, location, contacts, microphone or phone permissions. The advertising and Google SDKs add further entries to the app’s permission list as their documentation describes (for example Wi-Fi state, ad-services and install-referrer access, and storage on old Android versions); MixDocs never asks you for them and does not use them itself.

3. How Ads and Consent Work

Ads are provided through the Appodeal mediation platform and the advertising networks reachable through it (listed in Section 13). Before any ad is requested, MixDocs asks Appodeal’s Consent Management Platform together with Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) whether a consent prompt is required for you (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, or under US state laws), and shows it when needed. Your choice is respected by the networks: without consent, only non-personalized ads are requested where the law requires it.

The free version of MixDocs needs an internet connection so that ads can be shown; when you are offline it asks you to connect or to get MixDocs Pro, which works offline. It only reads whether the device is online, never the network’s traffic, and it never interrupts work you already started.

Where ads appear (free version only): banner and native ads, marked as ads, in the Files, Tools and Scan tabs and in folders; a medium rectangle ad on a tool’s progress and result screens; a full-screen ad when you leave the result of a completed free tool or close a document you have read for a while; and, only if you choose it, a rewarded video ad to use a Pro feature once. Ads never appear inside an open document, an editor, a tool’s settings, Settings or dialogs. The advertising SDKs process your device’s advertising ID, technical device and network information (including IP address, from which an approximate location may be derived) and ad interactions, under their own privacy policies. They never receive your documents or their contents.

MixDocs shows ads in its free version. Google AdMob and the other advertising partners named in this policy may serve ads, collect information directly from your device, and use your device’s advertising ID (and, inside the web content of an ad, cookies or similar technologies) to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure their performance, prevent fraud and, where you have consented or the law allows, personalize them. To learn how Google uses this information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can choose whether Google shows you personalized ads in My Ad Center, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads on some devices).

4. MixDocs Pro (Purchase)

MixDocs Pro is a one-time purchase that removes all ads and unlocks the advanced (Pro) features; the basic features stay free for everyone, and you can also watch a rewarded ad to use a Pro feature once. The purchase is processed by Google Play under Google’s terms. MixDocs never sees your payment details. To confirm that a purchase is genuine, the app sends the product identifier, the Google Play purchase receipt and its package name to our purchase-check service (Cloud Functions for Firebase, run by MixApplications), which asks Google Play whether the purchase is valid and answers only yes or no; nothing else is sent and the service keeps no copy. The app then keeps, on your device and encrypted, the Google Play purchase record (purchase token, receipt and Google’s signature) so that Pro stays active offline. Before a purchase MixDocs may ask Google Play for an age range (Play Age Signals) to apply Google Play’s rules for younger users; only the resulting setting is kept, on the device. When Pro is active the advertising SDKs are not started.

5. Optional Downloads

Some parts of MixDocs are delivered by Google Play separately from the app: the built-in text-recognition models are downloaded automatically right after installation, and the optional extra-language pack only when you ask for it. The download is made by Google Play, like an app update; MixDocs itself does not contact our servers or any other server for it.

Free fonts (optional). If a document uses a font your phone does not have and you tap “Get free fonts”, MixDocs asks Google Play services on your phone for that font by its name, and Google Play services downloads it from Google Fonts (Google’s handling is described in Google’s Privacy Policy). Only the font’s name is sent — never the document, its contents or its name — and nothing is sent unless you tap the button. Fonts you add yourself (Settings › Fonts) never leave your phone.

6. Data Security, Retention and Deletion

Security. Documents are processed in the app’s private storage on your device and are never uploaded. Temporary working copies stay in that private storage and are removed when the work ends (and at the next app start if the app was closed in the middle). Traffic of the Google Play, Firebase and advertising SDKs is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) wherever the ad network supports it. MixDocs has no account and keeps no copy of your data on any server.

Everything MixDocs itself stores lives on your device. Crash reports and usage statistics sent to Firebase are kept by Google for the retention periods set for our Firebase project (see Section 13). You can clear the app’s cache and recent-files list in the app, remove folders you added, or delete all app data by uninstalling MixDocs or using Android Settings → Apps → MixDocs → Storage → Clear storage. Files you saved are your own files and stay where you saved them.

Data collected by the advertising SDKs is retained by those providers according to their own policies (see Section 13). Because MixApplications does not receive identifiers from them, a deletion request for advertising data is best made directly to the provider; you can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android settings.

7. Your Choices and Controls

  • Ad privacy choices: open Settings → Ad privacy choices in MixDocs at any time to review or change your consent (the row is shown where the law in your region requires a consent choice, and not with MixDocs Pro, which shows no ads).
  • No ads at all: buy MixDocs Pro.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Notifications: turn them off in Android settings; long tasks still run.

  • App interactions — ad impressions and clicks, app launches seen by the ad SDKs.
  • Diagnostics — crash and performance data of the ad SDKs themselves.

MixDocs does not collect your files, documents, photos, scans, their text, contacts, messages, precise location, or personal information such as your name or email address.
