Privacy Policy — USB File Manager

This policy applies to the USB File Manager app (com.mixapplications.usbfilemanager) published by MixApplications. It is specific to USB File Manager; other MixApplications apps are covered by the general privacy policy. Where the two differ, this page applies to USB File Manager.

Last updated: October 6, 2026
The short version

USB File Manager reads and writes USB drives on your phone. Your files and the data on your drives are handled on your device and are never uploaded. File and folder names stay on your device too, except that an error report can occasionally include the path of a file that failed. The App has no account and no coins or tokens. What is collected comes from the services built into it: ads (with consent where the law requires it), Firebase Analytics (adults only, and only with your consent where the law requires it), crash reports, an update notice and a purchase check if you buy Pro. Pro has no ads and needs no internet connection.

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile app USB File Manager (the “App”), published by MixApplications ("we," "us," or "our"), handles information. By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Overview

The App reads and writes USB drives (and, on rooted phones, the internal SD card slot) directly on your device. The contents of your files, their file and folder names, and the data on your drives are handled on your device. We do not upload, collect or receive them; the only exception is that an error report can occasionally include the path of the file that failed (Section 13). A file goes to another app only when you choose Share or Open with. A shared file can only be read; with Open with, the app you pick can also save changes to the file on the drive. The App has no user accounts.

The App connects to the internet for the following, each described below:

  • Ads through the Appodeal mediation platform and the ad networks reachable through it, after consent where the law requires it (Sections 5 and 13). Not in Pro.
  • Firebase Analytics (app usage statistics). It stays off for anyone Google Play reports as under 18. Where your region requires consent (for example in the EEA and the UK), it follows the choices you make in the consent form, purpose by purpose: if you do not consent to analytics, no analytics data is collected, and nothing is collected until you have answered. Elsewhere it is switched on without a form. Pro users follow the same stored choices (Section 13).
  • Crash reports sent to Firebase Crashlytics when the App crashes or hits an error (Section 13).
  • Update notice. When the App starts, it asks Google Firebase Remote Config whether a newer version is available or required. Firebase receives a Firebase installation identifier and basic app and device details.
  • Time check. The App asks public internet time servers (such as Google’s, Apple’s, Microsoft’s, NIST’s and the NTP Pool’s) for the current date. The request contains no information about you; like any connection, it shows your IP address to that time server.
  • Purchase check if you buy Pro (Section 4).
  • Ad connection check (free version only). To tell you when an ad blocker or Private DNS stops ads from loading, the App tries to connect to a few advertising servers. Nothing is sent beyond the connection attempt.

2. Where Your Data Is

  • Your files and drives stay where they are: on the USB drive, the SD card or your phone. Phone folders are opened only through the Android folder picker, and each USB drive only after you allow it in Android’s access prompt.
  • Recent files (up to 200 entries with the drive name, volume label, file path, size and dates) are kept in the App’s private storage so you can reopen them. Clear history removes the list. It is excluded from Android backups.
  • Files you open or share with another app are read straight from the drive while that app uses them. A shared file is read-only; with Open with, the app you pick can also save changes to the file on the drive. On Android 7, or if that fails, the file is first copied to the App’s private cache, and the copy is deleted the next time the App starts.
  • Settings (theme, sort order, pane layout) are kept in app-private storage. Your Pro status and the record of your verified purchase are kept in encrypted app-private storage.

3. Permissions

The App asks for or declares only what its features need:

  • USB host: to read and write USB drives directly.
  • Foreground service (connected device) and notifications: to keep the App running while another app reads a file from your USB drive. The notification permission is asked just before a file is handed over.
  • Wake lock: to keep the phone awake during a file operation, for a limited time.
  • Internet, network state and network change: for ads, Firebase, Google Play billing, the connection check and the time check.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ad partners as described in Section 5.
  • Root access (optional, rooted phones only): to read the internal SD card slot. This is a prompt from your root manager, not an Android permission.

The Google libraries and ad SDKs in the App add their own standard entries, as their documentation describes: Google Play Billing adds the in-app purchase permission; the ad SDKs add Android’s advertising-services permissions (ad ID, attribution and topics), Wi-Fi state, vibration, a licence-check permission and, for some networks, a fraud-prevention attestation permission; Firebase adds access to the Google Play Install Referrer service and a Firebase Cloud Messaging receive permission (the App sends no push messages). The App requests no storage permission and no access to all files, and no location, contacts, camera, microphone, the list of installed apps, permission to install apps or to start at boot; those that libraries would add are removed from the App.

4. Pro Purchase

Pro is a one-time purchase: no ads, no internet connection needed, and no notice about ad blockers or Private DNS. It is processed by Google Play under the Google privacy policy. The App has no coins or tokens. Earlier versions (12.x) also sold coin packs and offered optional reward videos for coins, with the coin balance kept on your device; those are gone.

To confirm a new purchase, or to restore Pro with Restore purchases, the App sends the product identifier, the App’s package name and the Google-issued purchase token over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to our purchase check, a small program we run on Google Cloud (Firebase Cloud Functions). It asks Google Play whether the purchase is valid and, where needed, completes it with Google Play. Nothing else is sent: no name, email, file or drive data. As Google documents for Firebase, the call also carries your connection’s IP address and a Firebase installation identifier. We do not store the purchase token or the answer in a database. When an earlier version of the App uses the older form of our purchase check, the server also writes Google Play’s answer about that purchase (such as its order number, purchase time, state and country) to its logs, which we use to verify purchases and to check for refunds. Google Cloud keeps these logs, like its usual technical request logs, for 30 days. If it refuses a request because it could not confirm the copy of the App, the App instead checks on your device Google Play’s signature on the purchase (that it is for this App, for this purchase, and that the payment is complete); nothing is sent for that check. If it does not pass, nothing is granted and nothing you already own is taken away. Once confirmed, Pro works offline for up to 30 days before Google Play is asked again. Pro users still send crash reports, receive update notices and use the purchase check when online.

We never receive your payment card or banking details. Google provides us with transaction records (such as order number, product, price, date and country) in the Google Play Console, which we use only for accounting, tax and refunds.

5. How Ads and Consent Work

Ads (a banner, native ad cards on the Storage screen, in the Recent list, in the Operations and Properties sheets and on the operation screen, and full-screen ads at natural breaks) are provided through the Appodeal mediation platform and the advertising networks reachable through it (listed in Section 13). Before any ad SDK starts, the App asks for consent through Appodeal’s consent manager, with Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) as a fallback, where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), and records your choice. If you do not consent, ads are shown without personalisation. Pro users see no ads, and the ad SDKs do not start for them.

USB File Manager shows ads. Google AdMob and the other advertising partners named in this policy may serve ads, collect information directly from your device, and use your device’s advertising ID (and, inside the web content of an ad, cookies or similar technologies) to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure their performance, prevent fraud and, where you have consented or the law allows, personalize them. To learn how Google uses this information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can choose whether Google shows you personalized ads in My Ad Center, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads on some devices).

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not maintain a database of users.

  • On-device data (settings, recent files, Pro status, cached files and the data the consent and ad SDKs keep) is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data. Your purchase stays on your Google account, and Restore purchases brings Pro back. If Android backup or device-to-device transfer is used, Android 12 and later may include the App’s settings, its Pro record and the data the consent and ad SDKs keep (a backup is held by Google for you and managed in your Google account settings); recent files are excluded. Android 11 and earlier back up nothing.
  • Crashlytics data is retained for 90 days and Firebase Analytics data for 2 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Purchase checks are answered and not stored by us (Section 4).
  • Advertising data is retained by each advertising partner under its own policy (Section 13).
  • Purchase records are held by Google Play under Google’s policy; the transaction records we see in the Play Console are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires.

Deletion requests: we do not offer a data deletion service. We keep no user database and no account, so we hold no data from the App that we can find and delete for one person. Data collected through Firebase is deleted automatically (Crashlytics after 90 days, Analytics after 2 months). To limit advertising data, reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings, change your ad consent in the App (where it is shown for your region: ⋮ → Settings → Help & about → Privacy Settings), or contact the advertising partner directly (Section 13). Uninstalling the App or clearing its data removes what it keeps in its own storage on your device (see above).

12. Children and Audience

USB File Manager’s declared target audience on Google Play is users aged 18 and older. The App is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because an app store cannot prevent a younger user from installing an app, the App uses Google Play’s Age Signals API. Google Play — not the App — decides whether to share an age range, and may show its own prompt asking permission to do so.

  • If Google Play reports a user under 13, ad requests are tagged as child-directed and under the age of consent, ads are never personalised, and purchases are turned off.
  • If Google Play reports a user aged 13–17, ad requests are tagged as child-directed and under the age of consent, ads are never personalised, and purchases follow Google Play’s own parental controls.
  • If a supervised account has a pending or declined approval, ads are not personalised and purchases are turned off.
  • Firebase Analytics stays off for anyone reported as under 18.
  • If Google Play reports an adult, or shares nothing, the App uses its normal settings, where personalisation still depends on your consent (Section 6).

We receive only a broad age range, never a birth date or identity document. It is kept in memory for the current session only, is not stored, and is asked for again the next time the App starts. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us (Section 16) and we will delete it.

14. Security

Data sent by the App to Firebase, Google Play, our purchase check and the ad partners travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections; plain, unencrypted connections are blocked. Pro status and purchase records are kept in encrypted storage inside the App’s private folder, which other apps cannot read. Our purchase check keeps no database of users or purchases, so there is no store of personal data on our side that could be breached. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we limit what leaves your device to what is described in this policy.
