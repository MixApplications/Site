Privacy Policy — ChaoMe Theme Editor

This policy applies to the ChaoMe Theme Editor app (com.mixapplications.miuithemeeditor) published by MixApplications. It is specific to ChaoMe; other MixApplications apps have their own policies, listed in the general privacy policy. Where the two differ, this page applies to ChaoMe.

Last updated: October 5, 2026
The short version

ChaoMe builds MIUI and HyperOS themes on your phone. Your themes, projects, images, fonts and exported .mtz files never leave your device, and the App has no account or login and no server of ours. What is collected comes from the services built into the App: ads (with consent where the law requires it), Firebase Analytics (adults only, and only with your consent where the law requires it), crash reports, app-integrity checks, and Google Play when you buy PRO. The App’s Privacy menu lets you change your ad choices.

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile app ChaoMe Theme Editor (the “App”), published by MixApplications ("we," "us," or "our"), handles information. By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described here. ChaoMe is an unofficial app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Xiaomi; MIUI and HyperOS are trademarks of Xiaomi, named here only to describe the phones the App works with.

1. Overview

Everything you make in the App is built on your device: the themes and projects you edit, the images, fonts, sounds, videos, GIFs, zip and icon-pack files you pick, and the .mtz theme files you export. We do not upload, collect or receive any of it. The App has no user accounts and no server of ours.

The App connects to the internet for the following, each described below:

  • Ads through the Appodeal mediation platform and the ad networks reachable through it, with consent where the law requires it (Sections 5 and 13).
  • Firebase Analytics. It starts switched off, with every consent signal denied. Where your region requires consent (for example in the EEA and the UK), it follows the choices you make in the consent form, purpose by purpose: if you do not consent to analytics, no analytics data is collected. Elsewhere it follows the consent tool. It stays off if Google Play reports you are under 18 or you choose Do not sell or share my personal information (Section 13).
  • Crash reports sent to Firebase Crashlytics when the App crashes or hits an error (Section 13). The Appodeal SDK also sends its own crash reports to Appodeal.
  • App integrity: the App checks that it is a genuine, unmodified copy and that PRO was bought through Google Play. Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity is included for the same purpose.
  • Google Play for the one-time PRO purchase (Section 4) and the Play Age Signals API (Section 12).

2. Where Your Data Is

  • Projects, working files and the images you pick are kept in the App’s private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read.
  • Your theme library (the themes the App builds and the themes and parts you add to it) is kept in the App’s private storage. A library backup is a zip file saved where you choose with the Android file picker; it is never uploaded.
  • Exported themes (.mtz files) are saved to MIUI/theme on your phone’s storage, or to a folder you choose with the Android folder picker. That is shared storage: the files are yours and stay there after the App is uninstalled.
  • Themes you install with Shizuku on Android 11 and later are handed to the phone’s own Themes app, on your device. Shizuku is optional and runs locally; it sends nothing to us.
  • Sharing and opening themes. When you Share a library theme, a copy goes only to the app you pick. .mtz files you open with ChaoMe from other apps, and (with Shizuku) the themes already installed in the Themes app, are read on your device.
  • Settings, your PRO status and your Do not sell or share choice are kept in encrypted app-private storage on your device; the consent form’s record is kept by the consent tools in the App’s private storage.

3. Permissions

The App asks for or declares only what its features need:

  • Internet, network state and Wi-Fi state: for ads, Firebase, Google Play billing, and to check that the free version is online.
  • Set wallpaper: so that Set Wallpaper can apply a theme’s wallpaper directly.
  • Shizuku (optional): to place a theme in the Themes app’s list on Android 11 and later. Without it, the App still edits and exports themes. Shizuku is the only privileged route the App uses (Shizuku itself may be started with root).
  • Storage: only on Android 10 and older, to read and write theme files.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ad partners as described in Section 5.
  • App icons: to theme app icons, the App reads the names and icons of the apps shown in your launcher and of installed icon packs. This stays on your device.
  • Wake lock and vibration are also declared.

The Google libraries and ad SDKs in the App add their own standard entries, as their documentation describes: Google Play Billing adds the in-app purchase permission; the ad SDKs add Android’s advertising-services permissions (ad ID, attribution and topics), a licence-check permission (from the AppLovin SDK; the App does not use it), a foreground-service permission and, for Amazon ads, a fraud-prevention attestation permission; a library adds access to the Google Play Install Referrer service, which reports how the App was installed. The App does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, notifications, access to all files, access to all installed apps, or permission to install apps; those that libraries would add are removed from the App.

4. PRO Purchase

PRO is a one-time purchase that removes ads, lets you use the App offline and unlocks the advanced tools (the advanced editors: file browser, XML editor, raw keys and resource catalogue; lock screen options; the new lock screen with its depth, dark-mode, charging and fingerprint options; always-on display and power menu; MAML code with live preview; combining with parts of other themes; and converting a theme for another system version); every other editor, Theme check, .bak import, sharing and exporting, previews and undo/redo stay free. Free users who open a PRO tool see a short explanation; where Google Play reports an age that does not allow purchases, the tool shows as not available on this account and no purchase is offered. Purchases are processed by Google Play under the Google privacy policy. The App checks your purchase on your device with Google Play each time it starts or comes back to the screen. We have no purchase server: no purchase token is sent to us. We never receive your payment card or banking details. Google provides us with transaction records (such as order number, product, price, date and country) in the Google Play Console, which we use only for accounting, tax and refunds.

5. How Ads and Consent Work

Ads are provided through the Appodeal mediation platform and the advertising networks reachable through it (listed in Section 13). Before ads are requested, the App asks a consent management platform — Appodeal’s consent manager, with Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) as a fallback — whether a consent form is required for you (for example in the EEA and the UK) and records your choice. If you do not consent, ads are still shown, without personalisation, and the note before the form says so. Firebase Analytics and Google’s consent signals follow your choices purpose by purpose (Section 1). Ad SDKs may note when an app advertised to you is installed, to measure ads.

ChaoMe shows ads. Google AdMob and the other advertising partners named in this policy may serve ads, collect information directly from your device, and use your device’s advertising ID (and, inside the web content of an ad, cookies or similar technologies) to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure their performance, prevent fraud and, where you have consented or the law allows, personalize them. To learn how Google uses this information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can choose whether Google shows you personalized ads in My Ad Center, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads on some devices).

6. Your Choices and Controls

The App’s Privacy menu is under Settings → Privacy (in earlier versions, the Privacy icon in the toolbar). It always contains Privacy policy, which opens this page. The other two entries appear where your local law gives you that right:

  • Ad and data choices reopens the consent form, so you can change or withdraw your consent (for example in the EEA and the UK).
  • Do not sell or share my personal information opts you out of personalised ads where a US state opt-out right applies. Ads still appear but are not personalised using your advertising ID: the App sends Google’s restricted data processing signal and asks Appodeal for non-personalised ads, and Firebase Analytics is switched off. The entry shows its current state, and you can allow personalised ads again.

Wherever you are, you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings, choose ad personalisation in My Ad Center, or email us (Section 16). Buying PRO removes ads entirely; with PRO, Settings → Privacy shows only Privacy policy. Crash reports and, where allowed, Firebase Analytics continue with PRO.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not maintain a database of users.

  • On-device data (projects, the theme library, picked images and fonts, temporary files, settings, PRO status and consent choices) is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data. Exported .mtz files and library backups in shared folders, and themes installed into the Themes app, stay until you delete them. If Android backup is switched on for your Google account, Android may include your projects, theme library and the images and fonts you picked (within Android’s backup size limit) in your device backup, which Google holds for you and you manage in your Google account settings; settings, PRO status and privacy choices are excluded from backups and device transfers.
  • Crashlytics data is retained for 90 days and Firebase Analytics user- and event-level data for 2 months (our retention setting), then deleted automatically; aggregated reports without identifiers may be kept longer.
  • Advertising data is retained by each advertising partner under its own policy (Section 13).
  • Purchase records are held by Google Play under Google’s policy; the transaction records we see in the Play Console are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires.

Right to request deletion: to request deletion of data tied to SDK-level identifiers (such as your advertising ID), reset that identifier in your Android settings, or contact us using the details in Section 16.

12. Children and Audience

ChaoMe’s declared target audience on Google Play is users aged 18 and older. The App is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because an app store cannot prevent a younger user from installing an app, the App uses Google Play’s Age Signals API. Google Play — not the App — decides whether to share an age range, and may show its own prompt asking permission to do so.

  • If Google Play reports a user under 13, ad requests are tagged as child-directed (COPPA) in AdMob and Appodeal.
  • If Google Play reports a user aged 13–17, ad requests are tagged as under the age of consent in AdMob (not child-directed).
  • For anyone reported under 18, ads are never personalised, Firebase Analytics is off, and PRO is not offered in the App.
  • If a supervised account has a pending or declined parental approval, ads are not personalised and PRO is blocked.
  • If Google Play reports an adult, or shares nothing, the App uses its normal settings, where personalisation still depends on your consent and on the controls in Section 6.

We receive only a broad age range, never a birth date or identity document. It is kept in memory for the current session only, is not stored by us, and is asked for again the next time the App starts. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us (Section 16) and we will delete it.

14. Security

Data sent by the App to Firebase, Google Play and the ad partners travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Settings, PRO status and your Do not sell or share choice are kept in encrypted storage inside the App’s private folder (the consent tools keep their own record in the App’s private storage), which other apps cannot read. We run no server of our own for ChaoMe and keep no copy of your themes or files, so there is no account or database of ours that could be breached. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we limit what leaves your device to what is described in this policy.
